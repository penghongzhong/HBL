\paragraph{Conditional selected-clock mixed-source freezing.}
Assume one genuine physical Hartree orbit supplies
\[
q_s=4\mu,\quad 0<m\le\mu\le M,\quad
b=(1+4\mu)/(2q),\quad \ell_s=-b\ell,\quad t_s=\ell^2.
\]
For $h=s-s_0$, $H=1+4Mh/q_0$, $\alpha=1/(4M)$,
one derives, rather than assumes, the terminal time and scale estimates
\[
 T=t(s_0)+q_0\ell_0^2,\quad
 T-t(s)=q(s)\ell(s)^2\le q_0\ell_0^2 H^{-\alpha},\quad
 \ell(s)^2\le\frac{q_0\ell_0^2H^{-\alpha}}{q_0+4mh}.
\]
Suppose further that the regular physical radiation continues to
$T$ in $W^{1,\infty}(\dot H^1)$ with bounds $E,V$ on its
energy norm and strong time derivative. The original
inverse-square Hartree mixed channel
\[
 {\cal C}(z)=|x|^{-2}*
       \left(|x|^{-1}\operatorname{Re}z\,|z|^2\right)
\]
then satisfies the quantitative Hardy--Sobolev--HLS estimate
\[
 \boxed{
 \|2b(s)({\cal C}(z(t(s)))-{\cal C}(z(T)))\|_2
 \le
 \frac{3A_H(1+4M)E^2Vq_0\ell_0^2H^{-\alpha}}{q_0+4mh}
 =O(s^{-1-\alpha}).}
\]
For terminal Schwartz radiation, the scale-collision source,
after subtracting its nonzero frozen mixed channel and the
physical-time Hartree density derivative, is
$O(s^{-1-\delta})$ in $L^2$,
where $\delta=\min\{1/(4M),1/4+1/(16M)\}>0$.
This does not control those subtracted terms or the full four-region
nonlinear PDE residual, and the physical modulation trap and strong
terminal radiation are still unproved. This is a paper-only
conditional refinement, not a new Lean theorem or a closed
original environment; acceptance stays $11/13$. See
the corresponding detailed mathematical note in verification.
