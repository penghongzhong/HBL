\noindent\textbf{Scope.}
The statements below concern the actual three-dimensional inverse-square
quartic Hartree source, its origin-normalized Kummer Green integrals,
and the matched physical field.  They do not assert the still-open
full nonlinear physical parametrix or its $s^{-9/4}$ residual bound.

Let $a>0$, $L\ge 1$, $r_0=L/a\le B<R_K$,
and let $K$ denote the original origin-normalized Kummer basis.
For the actual physical source $\mathcal S_\HH$ put
\[
 F(r):=-a^2\mathcal S_\HH(ar),\qquad
 M_\HH:=C_{\mathrm{phys}}(B_z,A_z)\,\ell^{1/8}.
\]
The previously established source and normalized basis bounds read
\[
 |\mathcal S_\HH(y)|\le M_\HH(1+y)^{-19/8},\qquad
 |X(r)|+|Y(r)|\le 2G/r,\qquad r_0\le r\le B,
\]
where $X$ and $Y$ are the actual physical fundamental solutions
and the Wronskian, with its original normalization, satisfies
\[
 W(r)=r^{-2}e^{-ir^2/4},\qquad |W(r)^{-1}|=r^2.
\]
Consequently, for $Z\in\{X,Y\}$ and $r\in[r_0,B]$,
\begin{align*}
 \left|\frac{Z(r)F(r)}{W(r)}\right|
 &\le G a^2 M_\HH\,r(1+ar)^{-19/8}\\
 &\le G M_\HH a\,(ar)^{-11/8}.
\end{align*}
For every $\rho\in[r_0,B]$ define the \emph{original}, unswapped
Green coefficients
\[
 A(\rho)=\int_\rho^B\frac{X(r)F(r)}{W(r)}\,dr,\qquad
 B(\rho)=\int_{r_0}^{\rho}\frac{Y(r)F(r)}{W(r)}\,dr.
\]
The exact physical change of variables $x=ar$ gives
\begin{align*}
 |A(\rho)|
 &\le GM_\HH\int_{a\rho}^{aB}x^{-11/8}\,dx
 \le \frac83 GM_\HH L^{-3/8},\\
 |B(\rho)|
 &\le GM_\HH\int_L^{a\rho}x^{-11/8}\,dx
 \le \frac83 GM_\HH L^{-3/8},\\
 |A(\rho)|+|B(\rho)|
 &\le \frac{16}{3}GM_\HH L^{-3/8}.
\end{align*}
In particular, \emph{neither Green coefficient carries a positive
power of $a$}.  The estimate applies at every normalized radius,
not only at the upper interface.

With the genuine coupled compatibility obstruction $\sigma=0$,
the matched Kummer field is exactly
\[
 w(\rho)=Y(\rho)A(\rho)+X(\rho)\bigl(B(\rho)+\gamma\bigr),
 \qquad
 \gamma=\frac{Y(r_0)DU-Y'(r_0)U}{W(r_0)}.
\]
The first equality is a statement about the complete inhomogeneous
Green branch, not an indicator-supported outer repair.  The second
equality fixes the regular coefficient from the actual left Fredholm
Cauchy jet $(U,DU)$, rather than introducing a free amplitude.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Remaining quantitative distinction.}
The coefficient estimate cannot be multiplied by $|Y(\rho)|\le G/\rho$
and declared uniform as $r_0=L/a\to0$.
A source-and-left-jet fixed-annulus estimate instead gives
\[
 |w(\rho)|+|w'(\rho)|
 \le E,\qquad
 E:=2C^3\bigl((B-r_0)M+\lvert U\rvert+\lvert DU\rvert\bigr),
\]
where $C$ is the original \emph{annulus-dependent} basis budget
and $M$ bounds the original Kummer forcing on this annulus.
For $v(y)=w(y/a)$ one obtains
\[
 \int_L^{aB}y^2\lvert v'(y)\rvert^2\,dy
 \le a(B-r_0)(BE)^2,\qquad
 |v(y)|\le (1+aB)E(1+y^2)^{-1/2}.
\]
These last physical source-controlled energy and bubble inequalities
are separate Lean candidates at the checkpoint; the entire time-uniform
inner/Green/repair/remote field estimate does not follow until
the moving-origin basis, exact cancellations, and true time derivative
are controlled.

\begin{align*}
 J_-(r_0)
  &:=\frac{U X'(r_0)-DU\,X(r_0)}{W(r_0)},\\
 |J_-(r_0)|
  &\le C_{\mathrm{reg}}\,r_0\bigl(|U|+r_0|DU|\bigr),\\

These formulas use the actual origin-normalized basis, its regular
mode, and the exact two-channel Fredholm source; they do not
prescribe a Type--II scaling rate.

\noindent\textbf{Transport of the genuine compatible source moment.}
The exact compatibility equation at the left interface is
\[
 A(r_0)=J_-(r_0).
\]
For every $r\in[r_0,B]$, interval additivity of the original
Green source yields
\begin{equation}
 A(r)=J_-(r_0)-
  \int_{r_0}^{r}\frac{X(\rho)F(\rho)}{W(\rho)}\,d\rho.
 \label{eq:actual-singular-green-transport}
\end{equation}
The matched origin Green field is therefore exactly
\[
 w(r)
  =Y(r)\left[J_-(r_0)-
      \int_{r_0}^{r}\frac{X(\rho)F(\rho)}{W(\rho)}\,d\rho
    \right]
   +X(r)\left[\gamma+\int_{r_0}^{r}
      \frac{Y(\rho)F(\rho)}{W(\rho)}\,d\rho
     \right].
\]
The first integral vanishes at $r=r_0$; this is stronger than
estimating $A(r)$ by a global constant and is essential when
the physical singular mode is multiplied back in.
A genuine near-$r_0$ weighted local-moment estimate remains to be
established to convert the algebraic cancellation into uniform
full-field $H^1$ and Hartree source bounds.

\noindent\textbf{Sharper estimate for the \emph{product} with the singular Green mode.}
The preceding estimate \eqref{eq:actual-local-hartree-green-moment}
used the coarse bound $|X(r)|\le G/r$, although $X$ is the
\emph{regular} origin mode. The actual Frobenius jet improves it to
\[
 |X(r)|\le C_{\rm reg}:=1+(D+1)G B^2
 \quad(0<r\le B).
\]
Retaining this improvement \emph{before} estimating the source
density and using the exact physical Wronskian gives, with $y=ar\ge L\ge1$,
\begin{align*}
 \left|\frac{X(r)F(r)}{W(r)}\right|
 &\le C_{\rm reg}a^2 M_{\HH}r^2(1+ar)^{-19/8}\\
 &=C_{\rm reg}M_{\HH}y^2(1+y)^{-19/8}
 \le C_{\rm reg}M_{\HH}y^{-3/8}
 \le C_{\rm reg}M_{\HH}.
\end{align*}
Therefore, for all $L/a\le r\le B$,
\begin{align}
 \left|\int_{L/a}^r\frac{X(\rho)F(\rho)}{W(\rho)}d\rho\right|
 &\le C_{\rm reg}M_{\HH}(r-L/a),\\
 \left|Y(r)\int_{L/a}^r
     \frac{X(\rho)F(\rho)}{W(\rho)}d\rho\right|
 &\le G C_{\rm reg}M_{\HH}\frac{r-L/a}{r}
 \le G C_{\rm reg}M_{\HH}.
 \label{eq:true-hartree-singular-green-product}
\end{align}
This is the needed \emph{physical product bound}: the apparent positive
power of the Kummer scale $a$ disappears \emph{after the singular mode
is multiplied back in}. Since $M_{\HH}$ contains the independently
established factor $\ell^{1/8}$, the bound retains a genuine
positive smallness factor instead of an artificial $a$-loss.
The estimate remains a \emph{candidate} pending strict CI
\texttt{ActualHartreeSingularGreenPhysicalSourceGain.lean};
it must not be counted as accepted before kernel replay.

The remaining part of $Y(r)A(r)$ is $Y(r)J_-(r_0)$,
to be estimated from the real two-channel Fredholm source and the
Wronskian trace; this is a distinct candidate. Neither estimate
