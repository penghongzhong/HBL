\noindent\textbf{Scope.}
The statements below concern the actual three-dimensional inverse-square
quartic Hartree source, its origin-normalized Kummer Green integrals,
and the matched physical field.  They do not assert the still-open
full nonlinear physical parametrix or its $s^{-9/4}$ residual bound.

Let $a>0$, $L\ge 1$, $r_0=L/a\le B<R_K$,
and let $K$ denote the original origin-normalized Kummer basis.
For the actual physical source $\mathcal S_\HH$ put
\[
 F(r):=-a^2\mathcal S_\HH(ar),\qquad
 M_\HH:=C_{\mathrm{phys}}(B_z,A_z)\,\ell^{1/8}.
\]
The previously established source and normalized basis bounds read
\[
 |\mathcal S_\HH(y)|\le M_\HH(1+y)^{-19/8},\qquad
 |X(r)|+|Y(r)|\le 2G/r,\qquad r_0\le r\le B,
\]
where $X$ and $Y$ are the actual physical fundamental solutions
and the Wronskian, with its original normalization, satisfies
\[
 W(r)=r^{-2}e^{-ir^2/4},\qquad |W(r)^{-1}|=r^2.
\]
Consequently, for $Z\in\{X,Y\}$ and $r\in[r_0,B]$,
\begin{align*}
 \left|\frac{Z(r)F(r)}{W(r)}\right|
 &\le G a^2 M_\HH\,r(1+ar)^{-19/8}\\
 &\le G M_\HH a\,(ar)^{-11/8}.
\end{align*}
For every $\rho\in[r_0,B]$ define the \emph{original}, unswapped
Green coefficients
\[
 A(\rho)=\int_\rho^B\frac{X(r)F(r)}{W(r)}\,dr,\qquad
 B(\rho)=\int_{r_0}^{\rho}\frac{Y(r)F(r)}{W(r)}\,dr.
\]
The exact physical change of variables $x=ar$ gives
\begin{align*}
 |A(\rho)|
 &\le GM_\HH\int_{a\rho}^{aB}x^{-11/8}\,dx
 \le \frac83 GM_\HH L^{-3/8},\\
 |B(\rho)|
 &\le GM_\HH\int_L^{a\rho}x^{-11/8}\,dx
 \le \frac83 GM_\HH L^{-3/8},\\
 |A(\rho)|+|B(\rho)|
 &\le \frac{16}{3}GM_\HH L^{-3/8}.
\end{align*}
In particular, \emph{neither Green coefficient carries a positive
power of $a$}.  The estimate applies at every normalized radius,
not only at the upper interface.

With the genuine coupled compatibility obstruction $\sigma=0$,
the matched Kummer field is exactly
\[
 w(\rho)=Y(\rho)A(\rho)+X(\rho)\bigl(B(\rho)+\gamma\bigr),
 \qquad
 \gamma=\frac{Y(r_0)DU-Y'(r_0)U}{W(r_0)}.
\]
The first equality is a statement about the complete inhomogeneous
Green branch, not an indicator-supported outer repair.  The second
equality fixes the regular coefficient from the actual left Fredholm
Cauchy jet $(U,DU)$, rather than introducing a free amplitude.

\end{align*}

     \frac{X(\rho)F(\rho)}{W(\rho)}d\rho\right|
