\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Same-anchor backward overlap and fixed-domain source stability.}
The original matched Green solution already solves its classical Kummer
ODE at every positive point below its origin-basis radius, including points
strictly to the left of its Cauchy anchor.  Thus the physical restriction
to $[L/2,L]$ keeps the original anchor $L$, its actual Fredholm jets,
its source, and its compatibility matrix.  No replacement of $L$ by $L/2$
is required in that matrix.  This corrects the earlier proposed
re-anchoring prerequisite.  Changing the physical field by smoothing is
a different operation: its nonlocal feedback must still enter the next
actual compatibility equation.

Write
\[
 c_0=c_0(\mu,\theta),\qquad
 \mathcal K_{a,c_0}D
 =\Delta_rD+a^{-2}\left(\frac{ir}{2}\partial_rD+c_0D\right),
 \qquad \Delta_rD=D''+\frac2rD'.
\]
At a fixed time let $D=u-v$, where $u$ and $v$ are the actual inner
and matched outer fields, with $D(L)=D'(L)=0$.  The same statement applies
to the spectral-corrected outer field, since its correction has zero
left Cauchy jet.  On the fixed interval $J=[1/2,1]$ set
\[
 x=r/L,\qquad V(x)=D(Lx),\qquad k=L^2/a^2,\qquad
 f(x)=L^2(\mathcal K_{a,c_0}D)(Lx),\qquad e=\Delta_xV.
\]
These definitions use the actual operator source, not an independently
postulated envelope for $D$ or $D''$.  Direct differentiation gives
\begin{equation}
 e+k\left(\frac{ix}{2}V'+c_0V\right)=f,
 \qquad V(1)=V'(1)=0.
 \label{eq:fixed-domain-actual-kummer}
\end{equation}

Put $Y=L^2(J;x^2\,dx)$ and define the right-anchored radial Volterra inverse
\[
 (Te)(x)=\frac1x\int_x^1(y-x)y e(y)\,dy.
\]
For $Z=xTe$ one has
\[
 Z'=-\int_x^1y e(y)\,dy,\qquad Z''=xe,
 \qquad Z(1)=Z'(1)=0.
\]
The $L^1$ norms of the two one-sided integration kernels on an interval
of length $1/2$ are $1/2$ and $1/8$.  Young's inequality therefore yields
\[
 \|Z'\|_{L^2(J)}\le\tfrac12\|e\|_Y,
 \qquad \|Z\|_{L^2(J)}\le\tfrac18\|e\|_Y.
\]
Since $x(Te)'=Z'-Z/x$ and $1/x\le2$ on $J$,
\begin{equation}
 \|Te\|_Y\le\tfrac18\|e\|_Y,
 \qquad \|(Te)'\|_Y\le\tfrac34\|e\|_Y,
 \qquad \Delta_xTe=e.
 \label{eq:fixed-domain-volterra-bounds}
\end{equation}
These identities first hold for smooth $e$ and extend to all $e\in Y$
by the bounded integral operators and weak differentiation.  Conversely,
a function with radial Laplacian $e$ and the two zero traces at $1$
is $Te$: the difference has $Z''=0$ and two zero traces.

Define the bounded operator
\[
 B_{k,c_0}e=k\left(\frac{ix}{2}(Te)'+c_0Te\right),\qquad
 q=\frac{k(3+|c_0|)}8.
\]
It follows from \eqref{eq:fixed-domain-volterra-bounds} that
$\|B_{k,c_0}\|_{Y\to Y}\le q$.
When $q\le1/2$, the norm-convergent series
\[
 (I+B_{k,c_0})^{-1}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-B_{k,c_0})^n
\]
has norm at most $2$.  Equation \eqref{eq:fixed-domain-actual-kummer}
then has the unique zero-jet solution
\begin{equation}
 e=(I+B_{k,c_0})^{-1}f,\qquad V=Te,\qquad
 \|e\|_Y\le2\|f\|_Y.
 \label{eq:fixed-domain-source-inverse}
\end{equation}
In physical coordinates, with
$B_{\rm src}=\|\mathcal K_{a,c_0}D\|_{L^2([L/2,L];r^2dr)}$,
\[
 \|\Delta_rD\|_{L^2(r^2dr)}\le2B_{\rm src},\qquad
 \|D\|_{L^2(r^2dr)}\le\tfrac14L^2B_{\rm src},\qquad
 \|D'\|_{L^2(r^2dr)}\le\tfrac32LB_{\rm src}.
\]
In particular the moving left endpoint $L/2$ introduces no independent
inverse-radius loss into these estimates.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Parameters and actual time differentiation.}
For two parameter/source triples $(k_j,c_j,f_j)$ satisfying $q_j\le1/2$,
put $e_j=(I+B_{k_j,c_j})^{-1}f_j$.  Subtracting the equations gives
\[
 (I+B_{k_1,c_1})(e_1-e_2)
 =f_1-f_2-(B_{k_1,c_1}-B_{k_2,c_2})e_2.
\]
Consequently,
\begin{equation}
 \|e_1-e_2\|_Y
 \le2\|f_1-f_2\|_Y+
 4\left(\tfrac38|k_1-k_2|+
          \tfrac18|k_1c_1-k_2c_2|\right)\|f_2\|_Y.
 \label{eq:fixed-domain-source-lipschitz}
\end{equation}
This is a source-to-field estimate, not an assumption that the actual
nonlinear Hartree source is Lipschitz with a prescribed time order.

If the actual maps $f:I_s\to Y$, $k:I_s\to\R$ and
$kc_0:I_s\to\C$ are $C^1$, the same inverse identity and the difference
quotient in \eqref{eq:fixed-domain-source-lipschitz} prove that $e$ is
$C^1$ and
\[
 (I+B_{k,c_0})e_s=f_s-(B_{k,c_0})_s e.
\]
Hence
\begin{equation}
 \|e_s\|_Y\le2\|f_s\|_Y+
 \left(\tfrac32|k_s|+\tfrac12|(kc_0)_s|\right)\|f\|_Y.
 \label{eq:fixed-domain-time-source-bound}
\end{equation}
No derivative of a selected Kummer basis and no third spatial jet at a
moving interface occurs.  The differentiability and estimates of the
\emph{actual} $f$, $k$, and $kc_0$ must, however, still be obtained from
the physical source, parameter equations and unique compatibility
selection; they are not proved merely by this operator calculation.

\noindent\textbf{The genuine smoothing perturbation.}
Choose a fixed smooth $\chi$ on $J$ with $0\le\chi\le1$,
$\chi=1$ near $1/2$ and $\chi=0$ near $1$.  On $[L/2,L]$ replace
the old inner field $u$ by $v+\chi(u-v)$.  Relative to the old field,
the perturbation is $P=(\chi-1)D$, not $\chi D$.  Set
\[
 \eta=\chi-1,\quad M_j=\|\eta^{(j)}\|_{L^\infty(J)},\quad
 C_\nabla=\tfrac34+\tfrac18M_1,\quad
 C_\Delta=1+2M_1+\tfrac18M_2.
\]
Then $P(s,r)=\eta(r/L)V(s,r/L)$, extended by zero outside the shell.
The traces $V(1)=V'(1)=0$, together with $\eta=0$ near $1/2$, give
zero value and first derivative at both shell endpoints.  By the
product rule and \eqref{eq:fixed-domain-volterra-bounds},
\[
 \|P\|_{L^2(r^2dr)}\le\tfrac18L^{3/2}\|e\|_Y,\qquad
 \|P_r\|_{L^2(r^2dr)}\le L^{1/2}C_\nabla\|e\|_Y,
 \qquad
 \|\Delta_rP\|_{L^2(r^2dr)}\le L^{-1/2}C_\Delta\|e\|_Y.
\]
For $\alpha=L_s/L$, exact differentiation at fixed physical $r$ gives
\[
 P_s=\eta V_s-\alpha x(\eta'V+\eta V'),\qquad x=r/L.
\]
It follows that
\begin{align*}
 \|P_s\|_{L^2(r^2dr)}
 &\le L^{3/2}\left(\tfrac18\|e_s\|_Y+
        |\alpha|C_\nabla\|e\|_Y\right),\\
 \|\partial_rP_s\|_{L^2(r^2dr)}
 &\le L^{1/2}\left(C_\nabla\|e_s\|_Y+
        |\alpha|(C_\Delta+C_\nabla)\|e\|_Y\right).
\end{align*}
For the second estimate use
$\Phi=\eta V$ and
$\Phi'+x\Phi''=x\Delta_x\Phi-\Phi'$.
Thus only $e,e_s\in Y$ are needed; a curvature trace $e(1)$ is not
required.  These are estimates for the actual moving smoothing field,
not a formal cancellation of distributions at the interface.

\noindent\textbf{Nonlocal feedback of the same physical perturbation.}
For $U,P\in\dot H^1(\R^3)$ let
\[
 \mathcal N_H(U)=(\kappa_H|\cdot|^{-2}*|U|^4)|U|^2U,\qquad
 M=\|\nabla U\|_2+\|\nabla P\|_2.
\]
The Hardy--Littlewood--Sobolev map $L^{3/2}\to L^3$, H\"older's
inequality and $\dot H^1\subset L^6$ give, by the actual quartic and
cubic difference expansions,
\[
 \|\mathcal N_H(U+P)-\mathcal N_H(U)\|_{\dot H^{-1}}
 \le C_H M^6\|\nabla P\|_2.
\]
The radial estimates above enter the three-dimensional norm with factor
$\sqrt{4\pi}$.  If the actual trajectories $U,P$ are $C^1$ in
$\dot H^1$, differentiating the same polynomial convolution gives
\begin{align*}
 &\|\partial_s[\mathcal N_H(U+P)-\mathcal N_H(U)]\|_{\dot H^{-1}}\\
 &\qquad\le C_H\left(
 M^6\|\nabla P_s\|_2+
 M^5\|\nabla P\|_2\|\nabla U_s\|_2\right).
\end{align*}
With the real-linear first variation subtracted, put
$\mathcal Q_H(U,P)=\mathcal N_H(U+P)-\mathcal N_H(U)-D\mathcal N_H(U)P$.
The second-order integral remainder yields
\begin{align*}
 \|\mathcal Q_H(U,P)\|_{\dot H^{-1}}
 &\le C_H M^5\|\nabla P\|_2^2,\\
 \|\partial_s\mathcal Q_H(U,P)\|_{\dot H^{-1}}
 &\le C_H\left(
 M^5\|\nabla P\|_2\|\nabla P_s\|_2+
 M^4\|\nabla P\|_2^2\|\nabla U_s\|_2\right).
\end{align*}
These bounds include the full nonlocal perturbation, rather than only
the local cutoff Laplacian.  Their dual-energy topology is not, by
itself, the stronger original weighted residual topology.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Source substitution and precise remaining obligation.}
On the same backward overlap define, with the same genuine Hartree source,
\[
 \mathcal T=\mathcal K_{a,c_0}u+\mathcal S_H,\qquad
 \mathcal O=\mathcal K_{a,c_0}v+\mathcal S_H+a^{-2}\delta u_0,
\]
where $u_0$ is the actual uncorrected physical matched field and
$\delta$ is the actual spectral shift.  The exact corrected Kummer ODE
has $\mathcal O=0$ on its classical domain.  In general the identical
source cancels, giving the exact source in
\eqref{eq:fixed-domain-actual-kummer}:
\[
 f(s,x)=L^2\bigl(\mathcal T-\mathcal O+a^{-2}\delta u_0\bigr)(s,Lx).
\]
In particular the time derivative must be computed from this \emph{same}
expression with its moving argument.  Neither an independent $f_s$
envelope nor a preassigned positive time exponent has been introduced.
For the actual real two-channel selection $M_{\rm sel}p=b_{\rm sel}$,
any available differentiability must be used through
\[
 p_s=M_{\rm sel}^{-1}\bigl((b_{\rm sel})_s-(M_{\rm sel})_s p\bigr),
\]
not through a freely chosen speed derivative.  Smoothing changes the
nonlocal source and therefore must be propagated through this equation.
The original full weighted four-region $s^{-9/4}$ residual, its parameter
Lipschitz bound, finite recursion and dynamically derived blowup rate
remain separate, unaccepted obligations.
