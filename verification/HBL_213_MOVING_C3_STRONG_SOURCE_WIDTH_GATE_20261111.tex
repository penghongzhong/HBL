\paragraph{A sharp moving Hermite repair source-space gate.}
For the actual $C^1$ physical field's interface discrepancy $d_2,d_3$,
write $P=h^2d_2\phi_2((r-L)/h)+h^3d_3\phi_3((r-L)/h)$
on $L<r<L+h$, with the usual Hermite endpoint polynomials; only the full
physical field $W+P$, not $P$ alone, is $C^3$ across this interface. Exact polynomial integration gives
\[
 \begin{array}{c|ccc}
 j&\|\phi_j'\|_2^2&\|\phi_j''\|_2^2&\|\phi_j'''\|_2^2\\ \hline
 2&73/18018&50/231&100/7\\
 3&1/90090&2/3465&4/63
 \end{array}.
\]
With $N_j=\|\phi_j'\|_2$, $C_j=\|\phi_j''\|_2$,
$T_j=\|\phi_j'''\|_2$, the exact physical radial Laplacian gives
\[
 \Delta_rP=d_2\phi_2''+hd_3\phi_3''
   +\frac2r(hd_2\phi_2'+h^2d_3\phi_3'),\quad
 \|\Delta P\|_2\le \sqrt{4\pi}(L+h)\sqrt h
 \sum_{j=2}^3h^{j-2}|d_j|
  \left(C_j+\frac{2h}{L}N_j\right).
\]
At a moving left endpoint the time derivative has a singular part
$-L'd_2\delta_{r=L}$; it cancels the \emph{opposite} singular
part of $\partial_s\Delta_rW$ only after the original four-region
physical field and its repair are combined.
For the admissible test family $d_3=h'=d_2'=0$, $0<h\le L/10$,
the regular contribution satisfies
\[
 \|\Delta P\|_2\ge \tfrac12\sqrt{4\pi}C_2L\sqrt h\,|d_2|,
 \quad
 \|(\partial_s\Delta P)_{\rm reg}\|_2
 \ge\tfrac12\sqrt{4\pi}T_2
 \frac{L}{\sqrt h}|L'd_2|,
\]
hence
\[
 \|\Delta P\|_2\|(\partial_s\Delta P)_{\rm reg}\|_2
 \ge\pi C_2T_2 L^2|L'||d_2|^2,\qquad
 \frac{\|(\partial_s\Delta P)_{\rm reg}\|_2}{\|P_s\|_2}
 \ge\frac{5T_2}{11N_2}h^{-2}.
\]
Thus a narrow $C^3$ repair shell alone does not produce smallness
of the time-differentiated \emph{strong} PDE source, even though
$\|P_s\|_2$ becomes small. Actual PDE jet/velocity bounds and
whole-field cancellations remain necessary. This is an obstruction
to the geometry-only shortcut, not to the proposed Type-II
Hartree solution. All displayed bounds are paper-only.
