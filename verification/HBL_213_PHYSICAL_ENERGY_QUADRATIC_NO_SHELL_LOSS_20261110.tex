\paragraph{The full nonlinear energy remainder need not carry a shell-volume loss.}
All norms in this paragraph use Lebesgue measure on \(\mathbb R^3\).
Let
\[
 X=\dot H^1\cap L^6\cap L^\infty,\qquad
 \|f\|_X=\|\nabla f\|_2+\|f\|_6+\|f\|_\infty,
 \qquad \mathcal I\rho=\frac3{\pi^2}|x|^{-2}*\rho.
\]
Splitting the convolution into \(|x|\le1\) and \(|x|>1\) gives
\[
 \|\mathcal I(f_1f_2f_3f_4)\|_\infty
 \le C_0\prod_{j=1}^4\|f_j\|_X,
 \qquad C_0=\frac3{\pi^2}\left[4\pi+(4\pi/3)^{1/3}\right].
\]
Indeed the two kernel norms are respectively \(4\pi\) in \(L^1\)
and \((4\pi/3)^{1/3}\) in \(L^3\), while the quartic density is
in \(L^\infty\cap L^{3/2}\).  In the Fourier convention
\(\widehat f(\xi)=\int e^{-ix\cdot\xi}f(x)\,dx\),
\[
 \widehat{\nabla\mathcal I\rho}(\xi)
   =6i\xi|\xi|^{-1}\widehat\rho(\xi),\qquad
 \|\nabla\mathcal I\rho\|_2=6\|\rho\|_2.
\]
As \(\|f\|_8\le\|f\|_6^{3/4}\|f\|_\infty^{1/4}\),
the actual real seven-linear map
\[
 T(f_1,\ldots,f_7)=
 \mathcal I(f_1f_2\overline{f_3}\overline{f_4})
 f_5f_6\overline{f_7}
\]
satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \|\nabla T(f_1,\ldots,f_7)\|_2
 \le C_T\prod_{j=1}^7\|f_j\|_X,
 \qquad C_T=6+3C_0.
 \label{eq:hbl-full-hartree-energy-multilinear}
\end{equation}
The first constant controls differentiation of the genuine nonlocal
potential; the other three terms differentiate its exterior cubic
factor.  Thus no mixed density term is discarded.

Since \(\mathcal N(U)=T(U,\ldots,U)\), its real second derivative
has \(7\cdot6\) terms. Taylor's integral formula and
\eqref{eq:hbl-full-hartree-energy-multilinear} give
\begin{equation}
 \|\mathcal N(U+H)-\mathcal N(U)-D\mathcal N(U)[H]\|_{\dot H^1}
 \le21C_T(\|U\|_X+\|H\|_X)^5\|H\|_X^2.
 \label{eq:hbl-full-hartree-energy-quadratic}
\end{equation}
The same polynomial identity gives the corresponding correction
Lipschitz constant
\(42C_T(\|U\|_X+\|H\|_X+\|K\|_X)^5
(\|H\|_X+\|K\|_X)\), multiplying \(\|H-K\|_X\).
Changing the background adds at most
\(105C_T(\|U\|_X+\|V\|_X+\|K\|_X)^4
\|K\|_X^2\|U-V\|_X\).

For the same critical radiation
\(Z_\ell(x)=\sqrt\ell\,z(\ell x)\), \(0<\ell\le1\),
\[
 \|Z_\ell\|_6=\|z\|_6,\quad
 \|\nabla Z_\ell\|_2=\|\nabla z\|_2,\quad
 \|Z_\ell\|_\infty=\sqrt\ell\,\|z\|_\infty.
\]
Consequently the true background \(W+Z_\ell\) has a uniform
\(X\) bound whenever the physical radiation does.  If the actual
assembled correction were proved to obey
\(\|H(s)\|_X\lesssim s^{-9/8}\), then
\eqref{eq:hbl-full-hartree-energy-quadratic} would give
\(O(s^{-9/4})\) for its full nonlinear Taylor remainder, without
an extra shell factor.  With a separately proved bounded projected
energy propagator, its absolute time integral would be
\(O(s^{-5/4})\).  This is a conditional application of the
proved functional estimate, not a proved time budget of the trajectory.

The actual \(X\)-decay, the moving linear/cutoff residual, the projected
evolution inverse, and preservation of \(L^\infty\) by the final fixed
point remain open.  In particular a normalized Kummer energy bound
acquires a factor \(a\) in the physical squared norm under \(y=ar\):
\[
 \int_L^{aB}y^2|\partial_y w(y/a)|^2\,dy
  =a\int_{L/a}^B r^2|w'(r)|^2\,dr.
\]
This factor cannot be removed by relabelling a regional norm.
The estimate above is not the stronger weighted radial Kato-dual source
estimate and does not replace the Fredholm forcing interface.
It is a paper proof with no new Lean axiom or original environment
acceptance.  The revised count stays \(11/13\).
