\paragraph{The genuine Hartree-source regularity import gate.}
The strictly accepted source selector requires measurable radial radiation with
the pointwise Strauss bounds, whereas the classical three-\(C^1\) transmission
theorem additionally assumes positive-radius continuity of the forcing.
The latter does not follow from the former. At \(c=0\), take the positive
bubble \(V\), and a real radial radiation \(Z=t{\bf1}_{[R,2R]}\), \(t>0\).
Writing \(Q_H=|x|^{-2}*|H|^4\) and
\(\mathcal S_H(V,Z)=Q_{V+Z}(V+Z)^3-Q_VV^3-Q_ZZ^3\),
the three Riesz potentials are continuous, but at \(R\) the physical
source has the strictly positive jump
\[
 [\mathcal S_H]_R
 =Q_{V+Z}(R)((V(R)+t)^3-V(R)^3)-Q_Z(R)t^3
 \ge 3Q_Z(R)V(R)t(V(R)+t)>0.
\]
The admissible measurable choice \(z(y)=\varepsilon{\bf1}_{[1,2]}(y)\),
\(Z_\ell(r)=\sqrt\ell z(\ell r)\) puts this jump at \(r=\ell^{-1}\);
with suitable allowed \(L<\ell^{-1}<aB\), the scaled original source
\(F_a(r)=a^2\mathcal S_H(V,Z_\ell)(ar)\) is discontinuous inside the
Kummer annulus. This is a counterexample to automatic import of
\texttt{ContinuousAt F}, not a claim of irregularity for the smooth
orbit sought in the theorem.

A constructive bridge is available for sufficiently regular physical
radiation. If \(\rho\in L^1\cap L^\infty(\mathbb R^3)\) is locally
\(C^{0,\alpha}\), \(0<\alpha\le1\), on a unit neighborhood of a fixed
annulus \(A\), then \(P=|x|^{-2}*\rho\) is \(C^1\) on \(A\), with the
absolutely convergent compensated representation
\[
 \nabla P(x)=-2\int_{\mathbb R^3}
   \frac{x-y}{|x-y|^4}
   \left(\rho(y)-\rho(x){\bf1}_{|x-y|<1}\right)\,dy,
 \quad
 \|\nabla P\|_{L^\infty(A)}
 \le \frac{8\pi}{\alpha}[\rho]_{C^{0,\alpha}(A_1)}
       +2\|\rho\|_{L^1}.
\]
In particular, for a locally \(C^1\) physical field \(W\) with
\(|W|^4\in L^1\cap L^\infty\), the genuine nonlocal source
\(\mathcal N_H(W)=\kappa_H P_W|W|^2W\) belongs to \(C^1(A)\), and
\[
 \partial_r\mathcal N_H(W)=\kappa_H\left[
  (\partial_rP_W)|W|^2W+
  P_W\left(2\operatorname{Re}(\bar WW_r)W+|W|^2W_r\right)
 \right].
\]
The scaled physical source derivative carries the exact factor \(a^3\).
The needed \(C^1_r\) radiation bounds, their genuine temporal estimates,
moving selected speeds, nonlocal residual decay, and the finite recursion
are \emph{not} consequences of this local regularity lemma. This
calculation is paper-only, without a new Lean CI; the original revised
environment count remains \(11/13\).
