\subsection*{The actual moving singular Cramer feedback: a differentiated source producer}
The following is a paper-level estimate and is not claimed to be a newly audited Lean theorem.
Let $S$ be the homogeneous Sobolev constant and put
\[
c_*=(4\pi)^{-1/2},\qquad
C_*=(2\pi)^{-1}+(32\pi/3)^{1/3}S^4,\qquad
C_0=\kappa_Hc_*^3C_*.
\]
For radial energy fields, the seven-real-linear Hartree form
\[
\mathfrak T(a_1,\ldots,a_7)=
\kappa_H\bigl(|x|^{-2}*(a_1\bar a_2a_3\bar a_4)\bigr)
a_5\bar a_6a_7
\]
satisfies
\[
\sup_{R>0}R^{5/2}|\mathfrak T(a_1,\ldots,a_7)(R)|
\le C_0\prod_{j=1}^7\|\nabla a_j\|_2.
\]
Indeed the near convolution integral is $2\pi R$, the radial bound is
$|a_j(r)|\le c_*r^{-1/2}\|\nabla a_j\|_2$, and the far kernel
has $L^3$ norm $(32\pi/3)^{1/3}R^{-1}$.
For $U,V\in C^1(J;\dot H^1_{\rm rad})$ let
\[
f=\mathcal N_H(U)-\mathcal N_H(V),\quad
E=\|\nabla U\|_2+\|\nabla V\|_2,\quad
D=\|\nabla(U-V)\|_2,\quad
E_1=\|\nabla U_s\|_2+\|\nabla V_s\|_2,\quad
D_1=\|\nabla(U_s-V_s)\|_2.
\]
The seven slots and their six-slot differences imply
\[
\sup_R R^{5/2}|f(s,R)|\le7C_0 E^6D,\qquad
\sup_R R^{5/2}|\partial_sf(s,R)|
\le C_0(7E^6D_1+42E^5E_1D).
\]
For the original origin-normalized numerator $k(s,r)=K_s.y(r)$,
the exact change $y=ar$ in the actual singular-Wronskian coefficient gives
\[
\mathcal C(s)=\int_{L(s)}^{T(s)}
y\,k(s,y/a(s))e^{iy^2/(4a(s)^2)}f(s,y)\,dy .
\]
Writing $B(s,y)=k(s,y/a)e^{iy^2/(4a^2)}$ and $z=y/a$,
differentiation at fixed physical $y$ yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal C'={}&T'TB(s,T)f(s,T)-L'LB(s,L)f(s,L)
 +\int_L^T yB\,\partial_sf\,dy\\
&+\int_L^T ye^{iy^2/(4a^2)}f(s,y)
\left(\partial_sk(s,z)-\frac{a'}a z\partial_rk(s,z)
-i\frac{a'}a\frac{y^2}{2a^2}k(s,z)\right)dy .
\end{aligned}
\]
Set $A_0=\sup_{L\le y\le T}|k(s,y/a)|$,
$A_t=\sup_{L\le y\le T}|\partial_sk(s,y/a)|$ and
$A_r=\sup_{L\le y\le T}|(y/a)\partial_rk(s,y/a)|$.
Since $\int_L^T y^{-3/2}dy\le2L^{-1/2}$, the exact
source-derived bound is
\[
\begin{aligned}
|\mathcal C'|\le {}&
7C_0A_0E^6D\bigl(|L'|L^{-3/2}+|T'|T^{-3/2}\bigr)\\
&+2C_0A_0L^{-1/2}\bigl(7E^6D_1+42E^5E_1D\bigr)\\
&+14C_0E^6DL^{-1/2}
\left[A_t+\left|\frac{a'}a\right|
\left(A_r+\frac{T^2}{2a^2}A_0\right)\right].
\end{aligned}
\]
There is no negative power of the Kummer left radius $L/a$.
The quantities $A_t,A_r,E_1,D_1$ are \emph{not} independently assumed
small: they still must be produced at the necessary time order from
the same physical Hartree trajectory and its moving Kummer basis.
This controls only the nonlinear feedback increment, not the complete
four-region PDE residual. It does not close the original \S2.13 or
the final dynamically selected Type-II theorem; the count is $11/13$.
