\subsubsection*{Origin-conjugated moving spectral Green control}
For the frozen Kummer operator
\[
\mathcal L_{\mu,\theta}u=u_{rr}+
(2/r+ir/2)u_r+
\bigl(\theta+i(1/4-\mu)\bigr)u,
\]
let \(X=ru\) and \(\beta=\theta-i(\mu+1/4)\). The exact radial
conjugation is
\[
r\mathcal L_{\mu,\theta}u
=X_{rr}+\frac{ir}{2}X_r+\beta X.
\]
For the literal same-source matched Green field \(u_0\) and zero-left
spectral correction \(\mathcal L_su_1=\delta(s)u_0\), set \(X_j=ru_j\).
Then
\[
X_{1,rr}+\frac{ir}{2}X_{1,r}+\beta X_1=\delta X_0,
\qquad X_1(s,a(s))=X_{1,r}(s,a(s))=0.
\]
Fix \(0<a(s)\le B(s)\le B_*\), \(H=\sup_{[a,B]}|X_0|\),
\(M=1+B_*/2+|\beta|\), and \(E=\exp(MB_*)\). Gronwall for the
first-order system \((X_1,X_{1,r})\) gives the no-left-radius-loss estimate
\[
\sup_{[a,B]}(|X_1|+|X_{1,r}|)
\le E|\delta|H(B-a),\qquad
\sup_{[a,B]}(|ru_1|+|r^2u_{1,r}+ru_1|)
\le(1+B_*)E|\delta|H(B-a).
\]
Assuming classical mixed time derivatives on the actual swept
domain, the fixed-radius time equation and moving left Cauchy data are
\[
\begin{aligned}
(X_{1,s})_{rr}+\frac{ir}{2}(X_{1,s})_r+\beta X_{1,s}
 &=\delta'X_0+\delta X_{0,s}-\beta'X_1,\\
X_{1,s}(s,a)&=0,\qquad
X_{1,sr}(s,a)=-a'\delta X_0(s,a),
\qquad\beta'=\theta'-i\mu'.
\end{aligned}
\]
With \(\ell=B-a\) and \(H_s=\sup_{[a,B]}|X_{0,s}|\), the
same Volterra--Gronwall argument yields
\[
\sup_{[a,B]}(|X_{1,s}|+|X_{1,sr}|)
\le E\Bigl[|a'||\delta|H+
\ell\bigl(|\delta'|H+|\delta|H_s+
|\beta'|E|\delta|H\ell\bigr)\Bigr].
\]
The first matched profile has the \emph{derived} moving left data
\[
\begin{aligned}
X_{0,s}(s,a)&=a(U'-a'D),\\
X_{0,sr}(s,a)&=U'+aD'+a'D-
aa'[F_0(s,a)-(\theta+i(1/4-\mu))U-iaD/2],
\end{aligned}
\]
and the exact time source
\[
(X_{0,s})_{rr}+\frac{ir}{2}(X_{0,s})_r+\beta X_{0,s}
=rF_{0,s}-\beta'X_0.
\]
At the moving upper interface \(B(s)\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac{d}{ds}X_1(s,B)
 &=X_{1,s}(s,B)+B'X_{1,r}(s,B),\\
\frac{d}{ds}X_{1,r}(s,B)
 &=X_{1,sr}(s,B)+B'
 [-iBX_{1,r}(s,B)/2-\beta X_1(s,B)+\delta X_0(s,B)].
\end{aligned}
\]
The time derivative producers \(F_{0,s}\), \(\delta'\), \(\mu'\),
\(\theta'\), \(U'\), \(D'\), and moving endpoints must still be
derived from the unique same-orbit Hartree selector. These identities
neither presuppose nor establish positive time order of the full
nonlocal four-region PDE residual. They do not close the finite-profile
recursion or the dynamic Type-II rate; original revised acceptance
remains \(11/13\). This is a paper proof, not a Lean audit.
