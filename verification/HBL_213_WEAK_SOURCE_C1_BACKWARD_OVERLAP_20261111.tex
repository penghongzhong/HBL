\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Green--Wronskian regularization.}
On a compact positive annulus $[r_0,B]$, suppose the actual two Kummer
fundamental modes $X,Y$ are $C^2$, their exact Wronskian
$W=XY'-X'Y$ does not vanish, and the \emph{actual} Hartree forcing
$F$ is locally integrable. Define the unswapped Green coefficients
\[
 A(r)=\int_r^B\frac{X(t)F(t)}{W(t)}\,dt,\qquad
 D(r)=\int_{r_0}^r\frac{Y(t)F(t)}{W(t)}\,dt,\qquad
 w(r)=Y(r)A(r)+X(r)(D(r)+\gamma).
\]
Both coefficients are absolutely continuous. For almost every $r$,
the exact Wronskian cancellation yields
\[
\begin{split}
 w'(r)&=Y'(r)A(r)+X'(r)(D(r)+\gamma)
      +Y(r)A'(r)+X(r)D'(r)\\
 &=Y'(r)A(r)+X'(r)(D(r)+\gamma)=:H(r).
\end{split}
\]
Since $H$ is continuous, the fundamental theorem for absolutely
continuous functions implies
\[
 w(r_2)-w(r_1)=\int_{r_1}^{r_2}H(t)\,dt,\qquad
 w\in C^1([r_0,B]).
 \tag{WG-C1}
\]
Thus the true merely measurable, interval-integrable Hartree source
still produces a classical \emph{first} derivative at positive radii.
The jump-forcing example in the previous subsection obstructs
classical second derivatives, not the intrinsically regularized
first derivative. The zero-left spectral correction has forcing
$d\,w\in C^0$, and is likewise $C^1$. Replacing the stronger
\texttt{ContinuousAt F} hypotheses in the existing Lean
three-interface result by this integrable-source argument requires
a new, separately audited Lean declaration.

\noindent\textbf{Backwards smooth overlap in place of a $C^3$ shell.}
Let $L>0$, $I_L=[5L/8,L]$ and suppose the same physical Fredholm
inner field $u$ and corrected Kummer continuation $v$ belong to
$W^{2,2}(I_L)$ and have their \emph{actual} matched Cauchy pair at $L$.
Set
\[
 f=u-v,\qquad f(L)=f'(L)=0,\qquad
 g=\Delta_r f=f''+\frac2r f',\qquad
 \|h\|_{L^2_r(I_L)}^2=\int_{I_L}r^2|h(r)|^2\,dr.
\]
The exact radial flux and two backward Volterra integrations give
\[
 f'(r)=-\frac1{r^2}\int_r^L t^2g(t)\,dt,\qquad
 f(r)=-\int_r^L f'(t)\,dt,
\]
and hence
\[
 \|f'\|_{L^2_r}\le\frac{3L}{5}\|g\|_{L^2_r},
 \qquad
 \|f\|_{L^2_r}\le\frac{9L^2}{40}\|g\|_{L^2_r}.
 \tag{WG-V}
\]
These bounds have no inverse left-radius factor.

Use the already coded smooth transition
$\eta_L(r)=\chi(8r/L-4)$, equal to zero on $r\le5L/8$ and one
on $r\ge3L/4$. It has the physically correct orientation
\[
 U=(1-\eta_L)u+\eta_Lv=u-\eta_Lf.
\]
Writing $A_\chi=\|\chi'\|_\infty$ and
$B_\chi=\|\chi''\|_\infty$, direct differentiation and (WG-V)
prove
\[
 \left\|\Delta_rU-(1-\eta_L)\Delta_ru-\eta_L\Delta_rv
 \right\|_{L^2_r}
 \le
 \left(\frac{384}{25}A_\chi+\frac{72}{5}B_\chi\right)
 \|g\|_{L^2_r}.
 \tag{WG-L}
\]
For $L=L(s)\in C^1$ and real $b(s)$,
\[
 \|(\partial_s\eta_L+b r\partial_r\eta_L)f\|_{L^2_r}
 \le\frac{27}{20}A_\chi
 \bigl(L|L'|+L^2|b|\bigr)\|g\|_{L^2_r}.
 \tag{WG-T}
\]
The \emph{complete} inverse-square quartic Hartree nonlinearity
is retained in the following identity, valid in the corresponding
weak space for the globally defined full packet:
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal R_{b,\omega}(U)
 ={}&(1-\eta_L)\mathcal R_{b,\omega}(u)
 +\eta_L\mathcal R_{b,\omega}(v)\\
 &-2\eta_L'f'-(\eta_L''+2\eta_L'/r)f
 -i(\partial_s\eta_L+b r\eta_L')f\\
 &+\mathcal N_H(U)-(1-\eta_L)\mathcal N_H(u)
 -\eta_L\mathcal N_H(v).
\end{aligned}
 \tag{WG-PDE}
\]
No third spatial trace is needed for this smooth local interpolation,
and no singular moving-interface measure is introduced.
