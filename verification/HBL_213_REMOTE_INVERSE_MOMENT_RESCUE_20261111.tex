
\subsection*{Localized nonlocal density bound by the original inverse-square moment}
\noindent
For $L>0$, put $R=L/3$ and
$J=4\pi\int_0^R|\rho(q)|\,dq<\infty$.
The original radial inverse-square Hartree kernel outside its
inner density support has the absolutely convergent series
\[
 Q_\rho(r)=4\pi\sum_{j\ge0}
  \frac{\int_0^R q^{2j+2}\rho(q)\,dq}{(2j+1)r^{2j+2}},
 \qquad r>R.
\]
Since $|\int_0^R q^{2j+2}\rho|\le R^{2j+2}J/(4\pi)$,
$(2j+2)/(2j+1)\le2$ and
$(2j+2)(2j+3)/(2j+1)\le6(j+1)$, it follows that
\[
 |Q_\rho|\le\frac{R^2J}{r^2-R^2},\quad
 |Q'_\rho|\le\frac{2R^2J}{r(r^2-R^2)},\quad
 |Q''_\rho|\le\frac{6R^2J}{(r^2-R^2)^2}.
\]
Consequently, on the literal backward overlap $3L/4\le r\le L$,
\[
 \boxed{|Q_\rho|\le\frac{16J}{65},\quad
 |Q'_\rho|\le\frac{128J}{195L},\quad
 |Q''_\rho|\le\frac{13824J}{4225L^2}.}
\]
Thus a \emph{separate} volume-moment bound is not required for
this localized inner density, correcting the scope of the preceding
remote monopole discussion. For

\]
