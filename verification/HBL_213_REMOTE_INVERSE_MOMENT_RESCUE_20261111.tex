
\subsection*{Localized nonlocal density bound by the original inverse-square moment}
\noindent
For $L>0$, put $R=L/3$ and
$J=4\pi\int_0^R|\rho(q)|\,dq<\infty$.
The original radial inverse-square Hartree kernel outside its
inner density support has the absolutely convergent series
\[
 Q_\rho(r)=4\pi\sum_{j\ge0}
  \frac{\int_0^R q^{2j+2}\rho(q)\,dq}{(2j+1)r^{2j+2}},
 \qquad r>R.
\]
Since $|\int_0^R q^{2j+2}\rho|\le R^{2j+2}J/(4\pi)$,
$(2j+2)/(2j+1)\le2$ and
$(2j+2)(2j+3)/(2j+1)\le6(j+1)$, it follows that
\[
 |Q_\rho|\le\frac{R^2J}{r^2-R^2},\quad
 |Q'_\rho|\le\frac{2R^2J}{r(r^2-R^2)},\quad
 |Q''_\rho|\le\frac{6R^2J}{(r^2-R^2)^2}.
\]
Consequently, on the literal backward overlap $3L/4\le r\le L$,
\[
 \boxed{|Q_\rho|\le\frac{16J}{65},\quad
 |Q'_\rho|\le\frac{128J}{195L},\quad
 |Q''_\rho|\le\frac{13824J}{4225L^2}.}
\]
Thus a \emph{separate} volume-moment bound is not required for
this localized inner density, correcting the scope of the preceding
remote monopole discussion. For
$\rho=|u|^4-|v|^4$ and
$\mathfrak M(w)=4\pi\int_0^\infty|w(q)|^4\,dq$,
\[
 J\le4\big(\mathfrak M(u)^{1/4}
 +\mathfrak M(v)^{1/4}\big)^3\mathfrak M(u-v)^{1/4}.
\]
With the verified physical pullback
$Z_\ell(q)=\sqrt\ell\,z(\ell q)$,
$\mathfrak M(Z_\ell)=\ell\mathfrak M(z)$, and the
specific fields $u=V+Z_\ell$, $v=V$, this gives
\[
 \sup_{3L/4\le r\le L}|Q_{(|u|^4-|v|^4)\mathbf1_{[0,L/3]}}(r)|
 \le\frac{64}{65}
 \big[2\mathfrak M(V)^{1/4}
       +(\ell\mathfrak M(z))^{1/4}\big]^3
       (\ell\mathfrak M(z))^{1/4}.
\]
The $\ell^{1/4}$ factor is a spatial small-parameter gain,
not a proved temporal rate or a full spectral four-piece residual.

For a smooth cutoff $\chi(q/L(s))$, supported in $q\le L/3$,
write $C_\chi=\|\chi'\|_\infty$ and
$J_j=4\pi\int_0^{L/3}|\partial_s^j\rho|\,dq$, $j=0,1$.
Differentiation of the \emph{actual} density cutoff gives
\[
 \partial_s(\chi(q/L)\rho)
 =\chi(q/L)\partial_s\rho
 -\frac{L'}{L}\frac qL\chi'(q/L)\rho.
\]
The complete moving traces at $r=\theta L(s)$,
$3/4\le\theta\le1$ fixed, satisfy
\[
 \left|\frac{d}{ds}Q_L(s,\theta L(s))\right|
 \le\frac{16J_1}{65}+
 \frac{16C_\chi+128\theta}{195}
 \frac{|L'|}{L}J_0,
\]
\[
 \left|\frac{d}{ds}\partial_rQ_L(s,\theta L(s))\right|
 \le\frac{128J_1}{195L}+
 \left(\frac{128C_\chi}{585}
 +\frac{13824\theta}{4225}\right)\frac{|L'|}{L^2}J_0.
\]
These formulas require genuine physical differentiability.
Neither $J_1$ nor $L'$ has been assigned a decay rate;
the outer near-diagonal density and all remaining dynamic
strong-residual steps remain open. No new Lean CI or original
environment acceptance is claimed: $11/13$.
