\noindent\textbf{Radial energy-to-source bound for the actual quartic Hartree feedback.}
Let $u,v\in\dot H^1_{\rm rad}(\mathbb R^3;\mathbb C)$ and
\[
 \mathcal N_H(w)=\kappa_H (|x|^{-2}*|w|^4)|w|^2w,\quad
 E=\|\nabla u\|_2+\|\nabla v\|_2,\quad
 D=\|\nabla(u-v)\|_2.
\]
Let $S$ satisfy $\|w\|_6\le S\|\nabla w\|_2$ and set
\[
 C_*=\frac1{2\pi}+
       \left(\frac{32\pi}{3}\right)^{1/3}S^4,\qquad
 C_{\rm pt}=\frac{7\kappa_H C_*}{(4\pi)^{3/2}}.
\]
The radial fundamental-theorem estimate
$|w(R)|\le(4\pi R)^{-1/2}\|\nabla w\|_2$
holds for $R>0$. Split the potential integral at
$|x-y|=|x|/2$. The inner contribution follows from
$\int_{|z|\le R/2}|z|^{-2}dz=2\pi R$; the outer contribution
follows from
\[
 \|\mathbf1_{\{|z|>R/2\}}|z|^{-2}\|_3
    =\left(\frac{32\pi}{3}\right)^{1/3}R^{-1}.
\]
For $R=|x|$, the genuine convolution consequently satisfies
\[
 |(|x|^{-2}*|v|^4)(x)|
       \le C_*R^{-1}\|\nabla v\|_2^4,\qquad
 |(|x|^{-2}*(|u|^4-|v|^4))(x)|
       \le4C_*R^{-1}E^3D.
\]
Using the exact two-term nonlocal difference and the pointwise
cubic bound yields
\[
 \boxed{\;
 R^{5/2}|\mathcal N_H(u)(R)-\mathcal N_H(v)(R)|
       \le C_{\rm pt}E^6D,\quad R>0.
 \;}
\]
No pointwise source envelope was postulated; the bound is derived
from the physical radial $\dot H^1$ inputs and the true Hartree
convolution. The spatial exponent is invariant under the energy-critical
Hartree scaling.

\noindent\textbf{Original singular Kummer coefficient without compact shell support.}
Write $r_0=L/a$, $0<L<aB$, $a>0$, and
$F_a(r)=a^2[\mathcal N_H(u)-\mathcal N_H(v)](ar)$.
For the original Kummer singular mode $Y_K(r)=K.y(r)/r$
and the exact Wronskian $W_K(r)=r^{-2}e^{-ir^2/4}$,
put $C_Y=\sup_{r_0\le r\le B}|K.y(r)|$.
The original coefficient has the physical representation
\[
 \mathcal C_B^K[F_a]
 =\int_L^{aB}K.y(y/a)e^{iy^2/(4a^2)}
      y[\mathcal N_H(u)-\mathcal N_H(v)](y)\,dy.
\]
The preceding genuine nonlocal source estimate gives
\[
 \boxed{\;
 |\mathcal C_B^K[F_a]|
 \le2C_YC_{\rm pt}E^6D
       [L^{-1/2}-(aB)^{-1/2}]
 \le2C_YC_{\rm pt}E^6DL^{-1/2}.
 \;}
\]
This is independent of negative powers of the moving Green left
radius $r_0$, and it does not require a compactly supported repair.
For fixed $a,K,u,v$, varying only the physical endpoints
$L_j<T_j$, with $L_j,T_j\ge L_*>0$, also gives
\[
 |\mathcal C(L_1,T_1)-\mathcal C(L_2,T_2)|
 \le C_YC_{\rm pt}E^6D L_*^{-3/2}
 (|L_1-L_2|+|T_1-T_2|),
\]
provided $C_Y$ controls both integration intervals. This proves the
\emph{nonlinear endpoint} Lipschitz estimate directly from radial energy;
it does not prove the moving-Kummer-basis derivative, the true
Fredholm-jet speed derivative, or their parameter dependence.

\noindent\textbf{Unchanged original acceptance.}
The estimates concern only the actual quartic nonlocal Hartree
\emph{increment}. The complete original four-region PDE residual also
contains linear, spectral and time-differentiated terms whose
positive time order and parameter Lipschitz bounds remain unproved.
A $C_Y$ uniform in moving physical parameters and the actual energy
bounds for the selected profiles must be produced, not assumed.
This calculation has not been Lean-compiled, and original revised
acceptance remains $11/13$. Revised original \S2.13 and the
