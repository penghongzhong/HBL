\paragraph{The true mixed Hartree channel at energy regularity.}
For \(K(x)=|x|^{-2}\) on \(\mathbb R^3\), set
\[
 {\cal C}(z)=K*\bigl(|x|^{-1}\operatorname{Re}z\,|z|^2\bigr).
\]
Hardy's inequality \(\|z/|x|\|_2\le2\|\nabla z\|_2\),
homogeneous Sobolev \(\|z\|_6\le S_3\|\nabla z\|_2\), and
\(K*:L^{6/5}\to L^2\) give, with \(A_H=2C_{\rm HLS}S_3^2\),
\[
 \|{\cal C}(z)\|_2\le A_H\|\nabla z\|_2^3,
 \qquad
 \|{\cal C}(z)-{\cal C}(w)\|_2
 \le A_H\bigl(\|\nabla z\|_2^2+
 \|\nabla z\|_2\|\nabla w\|_2+\|\nabla w\|_2^2\bigr)
 \|\nabla(z-w)\|_2.
 \tag{HBL-E-LIP}
\]
The real Fr\'echet derivative, proved by polarizing the cubic
nonlinearity, is
\[
 D{\cal C}(z)[h]=K*\left(
 \frac{\operatorname{Re}h\,|z|^2+
  2\operatorname{Re}z\,\operatorname{Re}(\bar z h)}{|x|}
 \right),\qquad
 \|D{\cal C}(z)[h]\|_2\le3A_H\|\nabla z\|_2^2\|\nabla h\|_2.
\]
The exact critical dilation
\(z_\lambda(x)=\lambda^{1/2}z(\lambda x)\) satisfies
\(\mathcal C(z_\lambda)(x)=\lambda^{3/2}\mathcal C(z)(\lambda x)\),
so \(\|\mathcal C(z_\lambda)\|_2=\|\mathcal C(z)\|_2\).

\paragraph{Sharp distinction between fixed-orbit decay and uniform decay.}
For \(g_\ell(x)=\sqrt\ell(\ell^2+|x|^2)^{-1/2}\), define
\[
 \mathcal E_\ell(z)
 =K*\left[\ell^{1/2}\partial_\ell
 (|z+g_\ell|^4-g_\ell^4)
 -2|x|^{-1}\operatorname{Re}z\,|z|^2\right],
\]
where \(\partial_\ell\) holds \(z\) fixed. Differentiating
all four true quartic source monomials and using the same
Hardy--Sobolev--HLS factorization proves
\[
 \mathcal E_\ell(z)\to0\text{ in }L^2\quad
 \text{for each fixed }z\in\dot H^1(\mathbb R^3).
 \tag{HBL-E-POINT}
\]
This convergence is locally energy-Lipschitz uniformly in \(\ell\),
hence uniform on compact subsets of \(\dot H^1\). It is
\emph{not} uniform on bounded energy balls. Indeed, choose
\(0\le\psi\in C_c^\infty(\{2<|x|<3\})\), real, radial,
nonzero and sufficiently small in amplitude, and put
\(z_\ell(x)=\ell^{-1/2}\psi(x/\ell)\). Exact scaling gives
\[
 \|\nabla z_\ell\|_2=\|\nabla\psi\|_2,\qquad
 \mathcal E_\ell(z_\ell)(x)
 =\ell^{-3/2}\mathcal E_1(\psi)(x/\ell),\qquad
 \|\mathcal E_\ell(z_\ell)\|_2
 =\|\mathcal E_1(\psi)\|_2>0.
 \tag{HBL-E-NOUNI}
\]
The strict positivity follows directly from the linear term
\(12 k_{1,1}(r)g_1(r)^2\psi\), where
\(k_{1,1}=(r^2-1)/(2(1+r^2)^{3/2})>0\) on \(2<r<3\).
The exact global mixed covariant identity has the form
\[
 T_\ell^{-1}D_bQ+2b\mathcal C(z)
 =-b\mathcal E_\ell(z)
 +\ell^{3/2}K*\partial_t\rho_{\ell,z}.
\]
Consequently energy topology supplies a valid leading-channel
parameter Lipschitz estimate and qualitative fixed-orbit
static-scale convergence, but \emph{not} an energy-uniform scale
rate, a time derivative from energy continuity alone, or the
original positive-\(s\)-order four-zone PDE residual.
Quantitative selected-orbit regularity or genuine selected
Cramer/Kummer cancellation is still required. No new Lean
CI validates these analytic statements; original acceptance
remains \(11/13\).
