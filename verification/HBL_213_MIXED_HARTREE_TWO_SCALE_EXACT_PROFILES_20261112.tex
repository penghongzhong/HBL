\subsection*{The exact mixed Hartree covariant channel at fixed core radius}

Let $K(x)=|x|^{-2}$, $W(r)=(1+r^2)^{-1/2}$ and
$Z_{\ell,t}(x)=\sqrt{\ell}\,z(t,\ell x)$ for a smooth radial
Hartree trajectory satisfying
\[
 iz_t+\Delta z+\kappa_H(K*|z|^4)|z|^2z=0.
\]
For the asymptotic calculation alone assume $z(t)$ and $z_t(t)$
are Schwartz. Set
\[
 Q_{\ell,t}=K*(|W+Z_{\ell,t}|^4-W^4),\quad
 c=\operatorname{Re}z(t,0),\quad
 d=2|z(t,0)|^2+4c^2,\quad H=K*|z(t)|^4.
\]
The two \emph{exact} radial convolutions are
\[
 A(r):=(K*W^3)(r)
 =\frac{4\pi\operatorname{arsinh}r}{r\sqrt{1+r^2}},
 \qquad
 B(r):=(K*W^2)(r)=\frac{2\pi^2\arctan r}{r},
\]
where $A(0)=4\pi$ and $B(0)=2\pi^2$. The first formula follows by
integrating the radial inverse-square angular kernel by parts and
substituting $s=\sinh v$, $u=\tanh v$; the second follows from
$\widehat K=2\pi^2/|\xi|$ and
$\widehat{W^2}=2\pi^2 e^{-|\xi|}/|\xi|$.

The exact quartic expansion, dominated convergence in the mixed
quadratic density, and the untruncated scaling
$K*|Z_{\ell,t}|^4=\ell H(t,\ell\cdot)$ yield, uniformly for $x$ in
any fixed compact set,
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 Q_{\ell,t}(x)
 =4c\sqrt\ell\,A(|x|)
 +\ell\bigl[d B(|x|)+H(t,0)\bigr]+o(\ell).
 \quad}
\]
Under the same Schwartz control this expansion differentiates in
$\ell$ and $t$ with the corresponding little-$o$ remainder.
The cubic mixed contribution is
$O(\ell^{3/2}(1+|\log\ell|))$ on compact sets, while the linear
Taylor error is $O(\ell^{3/2})$.

For the original critical clock
$\ell_s=-b\ell$, $t_s=\ell^2$, put
$D_b=\partial_s+b(1+x\cdot\nabla)$. The resulting exact
two-scale covariant expansion is
\[
 D_bQ(s,x)
 =4bc\sqrt\ell\,\mathcal M(r)+b\ell d\,rB'(r)
 +o(|b|\ell)+O(\ell^{5/2}),\qquad r=|x|,
\]
where
\[
 \mathcal M(r)
 =\bigl(\tfrac12+r\partial_r\bigr)A(r)
 =4\pi\left[
 \frac{1}{1+r^2}
 -\frac{(1+3r^2)\operatorname{arsinh}r}
 {2r(1+r^2)^{3/2}}\right],
 \qquad \mathcal M(0)=2\pi.
\]
In particular,
\[
 D_bQ(s,0)=8\pi bc\sqrt\ell+o(|b|\ell)+O(\ell^{5/2}),\qquad
 \mathcal M(r)\sim\frac{4\pi}{r^2}
 \left(1-\frac32\log(2r)\right)\quad(r\to\infty).
\]
The spatially constant pure-radiation term $\ell H(t,0)$
cancels at order $b\ell$, but the spatially nonconstant mixed
$\sqrt\ell$ channel survives. A purely spatially constant
phase-frequency correction cannot cancel this profile on an
annulus; cancellation by the \emph{actual} two-speed selector
and moving Kummer correction remains an open obligation.

These expansions are \emph{fixed-core} statements, not global
$L^3$ estimates or statements on a radius $r\sim\ell^{-1}$.
They neither imply a positive renormalized-time residual order
nor establish a selected physical orbit, parameter Lipschitz
estimate, finite recursion, or dynamical Type-II rate. The
complete paper-level calculation is in
\texttt{verification/HBL\_213\_MIXED\_HARTREE\_TWO\_SCALE\_EXACT\_PROFILES\_20261112.md}.
The successful strict CIs 43135036481 and 43124289518 do not
verify this new estimate. The original revised count remains
\textbf{11/13}.
