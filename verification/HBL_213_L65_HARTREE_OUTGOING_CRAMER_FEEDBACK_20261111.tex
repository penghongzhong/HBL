\noindent\textbf{Exact regular Green--Hartree feedback.}
For $X_K(r)=K.x(r)/r$, $W_K(r)=r^{-2}e^{-ir^2/4}$, and
$C_X=1+(D+1)GB^2$ from the true regular-origin estimate,
\[
{\cal C}_A(F)=\int_{r_0}^B r^2X_K(r)e^{ir^2/4}F(r)\,dr,
\quad
|{\cal C}_A(F)|\le
\frac{C_XB^{1/2}}{4\pi}\left(\frac{4\pi}{3}\right)^{1/6}
\|F(|\cdot|)\|_{L^{6/5}(\mathbb R^3)}.
\]
For $F_a(r)=a^2({\cal N}_H(U+P)-{\cal N}_H(U))(ar)$,
HLS and Sobolev give
\[
|{\cal C}_A(F_a)|\le
\frac{7\kappa_H C_{\rm HLS}S^7 C_X B^{1/2}a^{-1/2}}{4\pi}
\left(\frac{4\pi}{3}\right)^{1/6}
(2\|\nabla U\|_2+\|\nabla P\|_2)^6\|\nabla P\|_2.
\]
No inverse left-radius loss appears. The singular Green coefficient
still has a sharp $r_0^{-1/2}$ $L^6$-dual-kernel cost,
and moving selection, full PDE residual and dynamics remain open.
This is a paper-only result; strict count $11/13$.
