\paragraph{Covariant untruncated Hartree flux under actual moving-scale transport.}
Let $K(x)=|x|^{-2}$, $\Lambda q=\frac12q+x\cdot\nabla q$,
$U=V+Z$, $\rho=|U|^4-|V|^4$, $Q=K*\rho$, and
$\Phi(q)=|q|^2\overline q$. Homogeneity gives
$K*(2\rho+x\cdot\nabla\rho)=(1+x\cdot\nabla)(K*\rho)$.
For the actual physical Hartree radiation
$Z=\sqrt\ell\,z(t,\ell x)$,
$\ell_s=-b\ell$, $t_s=\ell^2$, one has
$Z_s+b\Lambda Z=i(\Delta Z+\mathcal N_H(Z))$.
The full untruncated nonlocal flux is therefore
\[
 [\partial_s+b(1+x\cdot\nabla)]Q
 =\sum_{j=1}^3(\partial_j K)*J_j+K*R_{\rm cov},
 \qquad J=4\operatorname{Re}(i\Phi(U)\nabla Z),
\]
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 R_{\rm cov}={}&-4\operatorname{Re}(i\nabla\Phi(U)\cdot\nabla Z)
 +4\operatorname{Re}(i\Phi(U)\mathcal N_H(Z))\\
 &+4\operatorname{Re}((\Phi(U)-\Phi(V))(V_s+b\Lambda V)).
 \end{aligned}
\]
The previous standalone $b\Phi(U)\Lambda Z$ term cancels
exactly. Riesz multipliers and HLS imply
\[
 \|[\partial_s+b(1+x\cdot\nabla)]Q\|_2
 \le 2\pi^2\|J\|_2+C_{\rm HLS}\|R_{\rm cov}\|_{6/5}.
\]
For pure genuine radiation $H(t)=K*|z(t)|^4$,
\[
 [\partial_s+b(1+x\cdot\nabla)](K*|Z|^4)
 =\ell^3H_t(t,\ell x),\qquad
 \|[\partial_s+b(1+x\cdot\nabla)](K*|Z|^4)\|_2
 =\ell^{3/2}\|H_t(t)\|_2.
\]
This positive physical \emph{scale} factor does not imply the required
renormalized-time order. The genuine selected core's covariant
velocity, mixed nonlocal remainder, moving Kummer traces,
parameter-Lipschitz bounds, finite profile recursion and PDE-derived
Type-II law still require proof. The derivation is paper-only, not
Lean-certified; revised original acceptance remains $11/13$.
