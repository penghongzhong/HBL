\paragraph{Radial \(L^2\) time velocity directly controls the integrated genuine Hartree source.}
For radial \(h_1,h_2,h_3\in\dot H^1(\mathbb R^3)\) and
\(w\in L^2_{\mathrm{rad}}(\mathbb R^3)\), the exact angular integral
\[
 \int_{S^2}|Re_3-\rho\omega|^{-2}\,d\omega
 =\frac{2\pi}{R\rho}\log\frac{R+\rho}{|R-\rho|}
\]
and the source-radius split \(\rho\in[R/2,2R]\) and its complement give
\[
 \sup_{R>0} R^2
 \bigl|\,(|x|^{-2}*(h_1\bar h_2h_3w))(R)\,\bigr|
 \le C_{\rm rad}\|w\|_2\prod_{j=1}^3\|\nabla h_j\|_2,
\]
where, writing \(c_*=(4\pi)^{-1/2}\) and
\(\|h\|_6\le S\|\nabla h\|_2\),
\[
 C_{\rm rad}=4S^3+2^{3/2}\sqrt\pi\,c_*^3
 \left(\int_{1/2}^2
  \log^2\frac{1+t}{|1-t|}\,dt\right)^{1/2}.
\]
Indeed the complementary source radii contribute at most
\(4S^3R^{-2}\prod\|\nabla h_j\|_2\|w\|_2\).
