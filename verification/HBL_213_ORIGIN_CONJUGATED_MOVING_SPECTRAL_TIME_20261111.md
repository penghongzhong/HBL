## 1. Exact radial conjugation at the actual Kummer coefficients

At positive radius, the frozen physical operator is exactly
\[
\mathcal L_{\mu,\theta}u=u_{rr}+
\left(\frac2r+\frac{ir}{2}\right)u_r+
\left(\theta+i\left(\frac14-\mu\right)\right)u.
\]
Set \(\beta(s)=\theta(s)-i(\mu(s)+1/4)\) and \(X(s,r)=r\,u(s,r)\). Direct differentiation (all identities for a classical field) gives
\[
\boxed{\quad
r\mathcal L_{\mu(s),\theta(s)}u
=X_{rr}+\frac{ir}{2}X_r+\beta(s)X.
\quad}\tag{RO1}
\]
In particular, the original radial singularity \(2/r\) disappears. The coefficient \(\beta\) is *literally* \`originZeroCoeff mu theta\`; no new auxiliary Green operator has been postulated.

For the original same-source matched profile and its true zero-left spectral Cramer correction,
\[
\mathcal L_su_0=F_0,\qquad
\mathcal L_su_1=\delta(s)u_0,\qquad
u_1(s,a(s))=(u_1)_r(s,a(s))=0,
\]
write \(X_j=r u_j\). The exact regular-origin system is
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
X_{0,rr}+\tfrac{ir}{2}X_{0,r}+\beta X_0&=rF_0,\\
X_{1,rr}+\tfrac{ir}{2}X_{1,r}+\beta X_1&=\delta X_0,\\
X_1(s,a)=X_{1,r}(s,a)&=0.
\end{aligned}}\tag{RO2}
\]
This produces the **genuine** first spectral source \(\delta X_0\), rather than an independently bounded input.

## 2. Radius-uniform spatial estimate without differentiating Kummer modes

Fix \(s\), and let \(0<a=a(s)\le B(s)\le B_*\), \(B_*>0\).
Set \(M=1+B_*/2+|\beta(s)|\) and \(E=\exp(MB_*)\).
Writing \(Y=(X,X_r)\in\mathbb C^2\) in the \(\ell^1\) norm gives
\[
Y_r=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-\beta&-ir/2\end{pmatrix}Y+
\binom0{rF}.
\]
The induced matrix norm is bounded by \(M\), independently of \(a\).
By the elementary integral form of Grönwall,
\[
\|Y(r)\|_1\le
E\left(\|Y(a)\|_1+\int_a^r t|F(t)|\,dt\right).\tag{RO3}
\]
Assume only the existing source-derived first-profile bound
\(H=\sup_{a\le r\le B}|r u_0(r)|<\infty\).
Apply RO3 to the zero-left correction:
\[
\boxed{\sup_{[a,B]}(|X_1|+|X_{1,r}|)
\le E|\delta|H(B-a).}\tag{RO4}
\]
Since \(r u_1=X_1\), \(r^2(u_1)_r+r u_1=rX_{1,r}\),
\[
\boxed{\sup_{[a,B]}
\bigl(|r u_1|+|r^2(u_1)_r+r u_1|\bigr)
\le (1+B_*)E|\delta|H(B-a).}\tag{RO5}
\]
There is **no factor** \(a^{-1}\), no dependence on a singular Kummer mode bound, and no independent forcing envelope. RO5 is a distinct alternate derivation of the weighted value/slope interface already represented by \`ActualHartreeZeroLeftRegularizedVolterraSlope.lean\`.

## 3. Exact moving-time equation and the missing physical producers

Suppose \(a,\mu,\theta,\delta,F_0,u_0,u_1\) are differentiable in \(s\) with sufficient mixed spatial regularity on a swept positive-radius domain. Because the coefficient \(ir/2\) is time-independent, at **fixed** \(r\),
\[
\boxed{
(X_{1,s})_{rr}+\tfrac{ir}{2}(X_{1,s})_r+\beta X_{1,s}
=\delta'X_0+\delta X_{0,s}-\beta'X_1,\quad
\beta'=\theta'-i\mu'.}\tag{RO6}
\]
Differentiating the *actual* moving zero-left Cauchy data yields
\[
\boxed{X_{1,s}(s,a)=0,\qquad
X_{1,sr}(s,a)=-a'\delta X_0(s,a).}\tag{RO7}
\]
The nonzero slope is the same transported slope as SZ3, but RO7 contains **no inverse matching radius**: \(|a'\delta X_0(a)|\le |a'||\delta|H\).

Let \(H_s=\sup_{[a,B]}|X_{0,s}|\) (a quantity to be generated by the *actual* original PDE) and \(\ell=B-a\). Applying RO3 to RO6 with RO7 and then RO4 gives
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\sup_{[a,B]}(|X_{1,s}|+|X_{1,sr}|)
\le E\Big[
 |a'||\delta|H+
 \ell\{|\delta'|H+|\delta|H_s+
  |\beta'|E|\delta|H\ell\}\Big].
\end{aligned}}\tag{RO8}
\]
This is a **conditional derivative estimate**, not an assertion that its right-hand side decays in renormalized time. The only new quantities are genuine moving geometry \(a'\), spectral selection \(\delta'\), Kummer parameters \(\mu',\theta'\), and the original field velocity \(X_{0,s}\).

For completeness the original profile's true moving left data are
\[
X_0(s,a)=aU,\qquad X_{0,r}(s,a)=U+aD,
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
X_{0,s}(s,a)&=a(U'-a'D),\\
X_{0,sr}(s,a)&=U'+aD'+a'D\\
 &\quad-aa'\left[
 F_0(s,a)-\left(\theta+i(\tfrac14-\mu)\right)U
 -\tfrac{ia}{2}D\right].
\end{aligned}}\tag{RO9}
\]
The derivative PDE for \(X_{0,s}\) is
\[
(X_{0,s})_{rr}+\tfrac{ir}{2}(X_{0,s})_r+\beta X_{0,s}
=rF_{0,s}-\beta' X_0.\tag{RO10}
\]
Thus RO3 and RO9–RO10 reduce \(H_s\) to **actual** \(F_{0,s}\), \(U',D',a'\), and \(\beta'\), with no explicit negative power of \(a\). This is precisely where physical Hartree flux/selection estimates must enter; RO8 cannot replace them.

At a moving **upper** Kummer endpoint \(B(s)\), the exact traces are
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\frac d{ds}X_1(s,B(s))&=X_{1,s}(s,B)+B'X_{1,r}(s,B),\\
\frac d{ds}X_{1,r}(s,B(s))
 &=X_{1,sr}(s,B)+B'[-\tfrac{iB}{2}X_{1,r}(s,B)-\beta X_1(s,B)+\delta X_0(s,B)].
\end{aligned}}\tag{RO11}
\]
These identities avoid differentiating an explicit Kummer special-function basis. They also retain the moving-endpoint terms; \(C^1\) *spatial* interfaces alone do not control them.
