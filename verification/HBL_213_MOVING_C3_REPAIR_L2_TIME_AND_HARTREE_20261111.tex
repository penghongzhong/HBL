\paragraph{Moving third-jet repair and exact radial \(L^2\) time cost.}
At one actual C1 interface, take the original physical curvature
and third-jet discrepancies
$d_2=W_{rr}(L-)-W_{rr}(L+)$ and
$d_3=W_{rrr}(L-)-W_{rrr}(L+)$,
where the signs mean left minus right traces (not independent
repair parameters).
For $0<h\le L$ define on $[L,L+h]$
\[
 P=h^2d_2\phi_2(\xi)+h^3d_3\phi_3(\xi),\qquad
 \xi=(r-L)/h,\qquad
 \phi_2=\tfrac12\xi^2(1-\xi)^4(1+4\xi),\quad
 \phi_3=\tfrac16\xi^3(1-\xi)^4,
\]
and extend by zero elsewhere. The origin-side jet is
$(P,P_r,P_{rr},P_{rrr})(L+)=(0,0,d_2,d_3)$,
and all four jets vanish at $L+h$. At fixed physical $r$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 P_s={}&h^2d_2'\phi_2+h^3d_3'\phi_3
 +hh'd_2(2\phi_2-\xi\phi_2')
 +h^2h'd_3(3\phi_3-\xi\phi_3')\\
 &-L'(hd_2\phi_2'+h^2d_3\phi_3').
\end{aligned}
\]
Write $M_j=\|\phi_j\|_{L^2(0,1)}$,
$N_j=\|\phi_j'\|_{L^2(0,1)}$, and
$Q_j=\|j\phi_j-\xi\phi_j'\|_{L^2(0,1)}$.
Exact polynomial integration gives
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
j&M_j^2&N_j^2&Q_j^2\\\hline
2&43/180180&73/18018&20/9009\\
3&1/1621620&1/90090&4/405405
\end{array}.
\]
Define the \emph{derived} real physical budgets
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathfrak G&=\sqrt{4\pi}(L+h)\sqrt h\,
   (h|d_2|N_2+h^2|d_3|N_3),\\
\mathfrak V&=\sqrt{4\pi}(L+h)\sqrt h\big[
 h^2|d_2'|M_2+h^3|d_3'|M_3+
 |h'|(h|d_2|Q_2+h^2|d_3|Q_3)\\
 &\hspace{4cm}+|L'|(h|d_2|N_2+h^2|d_3|N_3)\big].
\end{aligned}
\]
Then $\|\nabla P\|_{L^2(\mathbb R^3)}\le\mathfrak G$,
$\|P_s\|_{L^2(\mathbb R^3)}\le\mathfrak V$;
there are no moving-support Dirac terms in $P_s$ because
$P$ vanishes at both shell ends.

\paragraph{Full nonlocal feedback of the moving repair.}
Let $W$ be the actual (unrepaired) radial physical field,
assumed at this step to have a genuine $L^2$ time velocity.
Put $E=2\|\nabla W\|_2+\mathfrak G$ and
$V_W=\|W_s\|_2$. For the *actual* inverse-square quartic
Hartree nonlinearity, the angularly integrated seven-linear
inequality (RV3) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\left|\int_L^{T_{\rm out}}rB(s,r)\partial_s
 [\mathcal N_H(W+P)-\mathcal N_H(W)](r)\,dr\right|
 \le 7\kappa_HA_0C_{\rm wk}L^{-3/2}
 [E^6\mathfrak V+6E^5(2V_W+\mathfrak V)\mathfrak G].
\end{aligned}
\]
The source is the whole-space nonlocal convolution, not a
spurious compactly supported source. The same exact physical
change of variables (MT3)--(MT4) yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
|\mathcal C_{\rm repair}'(s)|\le{}&
7C_0A_0E^6\mathfrak G
 (|L'|L^{-3/2}+|T_{\rm out}'|T_{\rm out}^{-3/2})\\
&+7\kappa_HA_0C_{\rm wk}L^{-3/2}
 [E^6\mathfrak V+6E^5(2V_W+\mathfrak V)\mathfrak G]\\
&+14C_0E^6\mathfrak G L^{-1/2}

\end{aligned}
\]
All moving jets, basis suprema and velocities in this estimate
must be \emph{produced} by the actual selected two-speed PDE orbit;
no positive time order is asserted.

\paragraph{Cancellation before differentiating the strong source.}
With right-minus-left jumps,
$[\Delta_rP]_L=d_2$ and $[\Delta_rW]_L=-d_2$.
Consequently the distributional time derivatives separately
contain $-L'd_2\delta_{r=L}$ and $+L'd_2\delta_{r=L}$,

$\partial_s\Delta_r(W+P)$.
