\noindent\textbf{Moving shrinking-splice obstruction for the genuine mixed Riesz source.}
Let $F_z=|x|^{-2}*(|x|^{-1}\operatorname{Re}z\,|z|^2)$,
$A_{\mu,\theta}=\frac i2r\partial_r+\theta+i(1/4-\mu)$,
$\mu>1/4$, $G=A_{\mu,\theta}^{-1}F_z$, and
$h_\ell(r)=\chi(r/\ell^\gamma)G(r)$, $0<\gamma<1$.
The fixed-source estimate for
$(A+\ell^2\Delta_{\rm rad})h_\ell-F_z$ cannot be made uniform
on $\dot H^1$ energy balls. Indeed, for nonzero nonnegative
radial $\psi\in C_c^\infty(\{2<|x|<3\})$ and
$z_\epsilon(x)=\epsilon^{-1/2}\psi(x/\epsilon)$,
$\epsilon=\ell^\gamma$, energy is invariant while the correction
vanishes for $r<\epsilon$. Hence
\[
 \|(A+\ell^2\Delta_{\rm rad})[
    \chi(r/\epsilon)A^{-1}F_{z_\epsilon}]-F_{z_\epsilon}\|_{L^2_{\rm rad}}
 \ge \|1_{r<1}F_\psi\|_{L^2_{\rm rad}}>0 .
\]
For the moving original Kummer radius, let
$\mathsf U_\ell f(r)=\ell^{3/2}f(\ell r)$,
$\ell_s=-b\ell$ and $\Lambda_{L^2}=\frac32+r\partial_r$.
Then the exact time derivative is
\[
 \partial_s(\mathsf U_\ell h_\ell)
   =\mathsf U_\ell[\partial_s h_\ell-b\Lambda_{L^2}h_\ell].
\]
If $z=z(t(s))$ with $t_s=\ell^2$ and the spectral parameters
$\mu,\theta$ move, the source-dependent inverse obeys
\[
 \partial_s h_\ell
 =\chi_\epsilon A^{-1}
   [\ell^2 D F_z[z_t]-(\theta_s-i\mu_s)G]
 +\gamma b(r/\epsilon)\chi'(r/\epsilon)G .
\]
For any fixed smooth nonzero $z$, the last moving-cutoff contribution
vanishes in radial $L^2$ as $\epsilon\downarrow0$, but
$\Lambda_{L^2}h_\ell\to\Lambda_{L^2}G\ne0$ in radial $L^2$.
Thus the spatial Kummer splice gain alone does not control the
moving-time PDE defect: the original Cramer modulation and entire
covariant four-piece operator must cancel or bound the order-$b$
dilation term. This is a paper-only barrier; no new Lean target,
no original environment closure, and the accepted count stays $11/13$.
