\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Singular Green feedback of the physical shell repair.}
Let $P\in\dot H^1_{\rm rad}(\mathbb R^3)$ be supported in the
actual physical shell $L\le r\le L+h$, $L,h>0$, and set
$F_a(r)=a^2\{\mathcal N_H(U+P)-\mathcal N_H(U)\}(ar)$.
Write
$E=\|\nabla U\|_2$, $A=2\|U\|_6+\|P\|_6$,
$D=\|P\|_6$, $V_{L,h}=4\pi((L+h)^3-L^3)/3$,
and $C_Y=\sup_{0\le r\le B}|K.y(r)|$.
The actual singular Kummer coefficient satisfies
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\left|\int_{r_0}^{B}\frac{Y_K(r)F_a(r)}{W_K(r)}\,dr\right|
\le C_Y\kappa_H\bigg[
 &\frac{16\sqrt2}{(4\pi)^{3/2}}
  E^3 L^{-3/2} V_{L,h}^{1/3} A^3D\\
 &+\frac{7C_{\rm HLS}}{4\pi}
  \left(\frac{32\pi}{3}\right)^{1/6}L^{-1/2}A^6D
\bigg].
\end{aligned}}
\]
Indeed, $\delta\rho=|U+P|^4-|U|^4$ is shell supported and
$\|\delta\rho\|_1\le4V_{L,h}^{1/3}A^3D$.
For $r\le L/2$, the genuine nonlocal potential change is at
most $4L^{-2}\|\delta\rho\|_1$; the radial energy inequality
$|U(r)|\le(4\pi r)^{-1/2}\|\nabla U\|_2$
then controls $\int_0^{L/2}r|\delta\mathcal N_H(r)|dr$.
For $r\ge L/2$, HLS, H\"older and
$\|1_{|x|\ge L/2}|x|^{-1}\|_6
 =(32\pi/(3L^3))^{1/6}$
give the other term. Finally $y=ar$ cancels both powers of $a$
in the original Wronskian coefficient exactly. This controls
the nonlocal change even inside the shell, with no $r_0^{-1/2}$
loss; it does not claim that the unperturbed source is shell supported.
For the Hermite repair, $D\le V_{L,h}^{1/6}
(h^2|d_2|M_2+h^3|d_3|M_3)$.
When $0<h\le L$, the exact shell volume satisfies
$V_{L,h}^{1/3}\le (28\pi/3)^{1/3}L$, so both terms above
gain at least $L^{-1/2}$ with a fixed-energy physical source.
For $L=\ell^{-1/16}$ this is a geometric $\ell^{1/32}$
factor only; no time rate or original residual gain follows.
The moving $C_Y$, real selected-speed Jacobian,
time/parameter derivatives, full nonlinear PDE residual and
Type-II dynamics remain unproved. Paper only, strict original 11/13.
