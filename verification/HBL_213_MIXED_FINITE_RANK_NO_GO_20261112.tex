\subsection*{The genuine Hartree mixed channel is not removable by two modulation directions alone}

Let \(K(x)=|x|^{-2}\) on \(\R^3\) and
\[
 {\cal C}(z)=K*\bigl(|x|^{-1}\operatorname{Re}z\,|z|^2\bigr),
 \qquad z\in\dot H^1(\R^3;\C).
\]
Hardy's inequality, Sobolev and HLS give
\(\|{\cal C}(z)\|_2\le 2C_{\rm HLS}S_3^2\|\nabla z\|_2^3\).
Choose nonzero nonnegative radial \(\phi_1,\phi_2,\phi_3\in C_c^\infty(\R^3)\)
supported in pairwise disjoint annuli bounded away from the origin, and put
\[
 f_j=|x|^{-1}\phi_j^3,\qquad g_j=K*f_j.
\]
The genuine Riesz multiplier \(\widehat K(\xi)=2\pi^2/|\xi|\)
implies that the three \(g_j\) are linearly independent in \(L^2\):
from \(\sum_j a_jg_j=0\), multiply Fourier transforms by \(|\xi|\),
invert the transform, and use the disjoint supports of \(f_j\).
Hence the Gram matrix \(G_{jk}=\langle g_j,g_k\rangle_{L^2}\)
is positive definite. Put \(\gamma=\sqrt{\lambda_{\min}(G)/3}>0\).

For \emph{every} real linear subspace \(V\subset L^2\) with \(\dim V\le2\),
\[
 \boxed{\max_{1\le j\le3}\operatorname{dist}_{L^2}(g_j,V)\ge\gamma.}
\]
Indeed the projection \(P_V:\operatorname{span}\{g_1,g_2,g_3\}\to V\)
has a nonzero kernel; for a unit coefficient vector \(a\) in this
kernel,
\[
 \sqrt{\lambda_{\min}(G)}
 \le\|\sum_j a_jg_j\|_2
 =\|\sum_j a_j(I-P_V)g_j\|_2
 \le\sqrt3\max_j\|(I-P_V)g_j\|_2.
\]
With \(z_{\ell,j}(x)=\ell^{-1/2}\phi_j(x/\ell)\) and unitary
\(U_\ell f(x)=\ell^{-3/2}f(x/\ell)\), exact Hartree scaling yields
\[
 \|\nabla z_{\ell,j}\|_2=\|\nabla\phi_j\|_2,\qquad
 {\cal C}(z_{\ell,j})=U_\ell g_j,\qquad
 \boxed{\max_j\operatorname{dist}_{L^2}
   ({\cal C}(z_{\ell,j}),V_\ell)\ge\gamma}
\]
for every \emph{shared} family of subspaces \(\dim V_\ell\le2\),
even when \(V_\ell\) depends on \(\ell\).
Thus a rank-two Cramer modulation selection, without its
source-dependent nonresonant Green/PDE correction, cannot uniformly
cancel the entire genuine cubic mixed channel on an energy ball.
This does \emph{not} forbid cancellation on a specially selected
Hartree trajectory, nor the infinite-dimensional Green response,
and gives no positive-time remainder bound.
It is an analysis-only gate, not a Lean-accepted statement;
the original revised count remains \(11/13\).
