\subsection*{A mixed core--radiation obstruction to the pure-flux scale shortcut}
The actual three-dimensional inverse-square potential at the origin satisfies
\[
 (|x|^{-2}*f)(0)=4\pi\int_0^\infty f(r)\,dr
\]
for radial integrable densities. Let
\(W(r)=(1+r^2)^{-1/2}\), \(Z_{\ell,t}(r)=\ell^{1/2}z(t,\ell r)\),
and \(Q_{\ell,t}=|x|^{-2}*(|W+Z_{\ell,t}|^4-W^4)\).
Assume the genuine Hartree radiation \(z\) and its physical time derivative
are locally uniformly radial Schwartz, so all derivatives below are
justified. The complete quartic density difference, including the mixed
linear term \(4W^3\operatorname{Re}Z_{\ell,t}\), gives
\[
 Q_{\ell,t}(0)=16\pi\sqrt{\ell}\operatorname{Re}z(t,0)+O_t(\ell),
 \qquad
 \ell\partial_\ell Q_{\ell,t}(0)
 =8\pi\sqrt{\ell}\operatorname{Re}z(t,0)+O_t(\ell).
\]
Indeed, \(\int_0^\infty W^3dr=1\), \(\int_0^\infty rW^3dr=1\),
and \(W^2\in L^1(dr)\), while the cubic and pure-radiation terms are
\(O(\ell^{3/2}(1+|\log\ell|))\) and \(O(\ell)\), respectively.
For \(\ell_s=-b\ell\), \(t_s=\ell^2\), set
\(D_b=\partial_s+b(1+x\cdot\nabla)\). Since
\(\partial_tQ_{\ell,t}(0)
 =16\pi\sqrt{\ell}\operatorname{Re}z_t(t,0)+O_t(\ell)\),
the exact chain rule yields
\[
 \boxed{\;
 (D_bQ)(s,0)=8\pi b(s)\sqrt{\ell(s)}
      \operatorname{Re}z(t(s),0)
  +O_t\!\left(|b(s)|\ell(s)+\ell(s)^{5/2}\right).\;}
\]
The original Hartree coupling constant multiplies this identity.
The pure-radiation potential instead satisfies
\(D_b(|x|^{-2}*|Z|^4)
 =\ell^3\partial_t(|x|^{-2}*|z|^4)(t,\ell x)\),
whose \(L^2_x\) scale is \(\ell^{3/2}\). This latter gain does
\emph{not} automatically pass to the mixed core--radiation potential.
The pointwise calculation is not an estimate in the original
four-piece weighted forcing space and does not exclude cancellation
from the genuinely selected modulation equation. Such cancellation
must be proved from the same physical Cramer/Kummer orbit rather
than assumed. A full derivation is in
\texttt{HBL\_213\_COVARIANT\_MIXED\_CORE\_OBSTRUCTION\_20261112.md}.
This is a paper-only quantitative obstruction; revised original
strict acceptance remains \(11/13\).
