\paragraph{A fixed interval for the actual moving backward overlap.}
Let
\[
 K_{a,Q}e=e_{yy}+\left(\frac2y+\frac{iy}{2a^2}\right)e_y
              +\frac{Q}{a^2}e,
 \qquad Q=\mathrm{frozenZeroCoeff}(\mu,\theta).
\]
For the genuine source-matched difference, put
\(E(x)=e(Lx)\), \(k=L^2/a^2\), \(z=kQ\), and
\(G(x)=L^2(K_{a,Q}e)(Lx)\).  The original physical
operator, not an independent model inverse, gives
\begin{equation}
 E_{xx}+\left(\frac2x+\frac{ikx}{2}\right)E_x+zE=G,
 \qquad E(1)=E_x(1)=0,
 \qquad x\in J=[1/2,1].
 \label{eq:hbl-normalized-moving-original}
\end{equation}
The effective Kummer scale is \(a/L\).  The already proved radial
weak-term absorption estimate, at \(R=1/2\), implies for
\(4k(1+|Q|)\le1\)
\[
 \|E\|_\infty\le2\|G\|_\infty,\qquad
 \|E_x\|_\infty\le4\|G\|_\infty,\qquad
 \|E_{xx}\|_\infty\le18\|G\|_\infty.
\]
Here and below all suprema are on \(J\).  The last estimate follows
from \(\|E_{xx}+2E_x/x\|_\infty\le2\|G\|_\infty\) and
\(2/x\le4\); it requires no independent inverse budget.

For two actual fields, possibly with different physical radii,
normalize each as in \eqref{eq:hbl-normalized-moving-original}.
Writing \(D=E_1-E_2\), their original equations yield
\[
 K_{k_1,z_1}D
 =G_1-G_2-\frac{ix}{2}(k_1-k_2)(E_2)_x-(z_1-z_2)E_2.
\]
Consequently, with the explicitly source-dependent quantity
\[
 B=\|G_1-G_2\|_\infty+
       2\bigl(|k_1-k_2|+|z_1-z_2|\bigr)\|G_2\|_\infty,
\]
the same inverse proves
\begin{equation}
 \|D\|_\infty\le2B,\qquad
 \|D_x\|_\infty\le4B,\qquad
 \|D_{xx}\|_\infty\le18B.
 \label{eq:hbl-normalized-moving-C2}
\end{equation}
Thus \eqref{eq:hbl-normalized-moving-C2} replaces repeated
moving-basis differentiation on this local overlap by a source-exact
coefficient-stability estimate.  Its source norms are literal suprema
of the original operator evaluations, not postulated envelopes.

For a sufficiently regular actual parameter family, differentiating
\eqref{eq:hbl-normalized-moving-original} gives
\[
 K_{k,z}E_s=G_s-\frac{ix}{2}k_sE_x-z_sE,
 \qquad E_s(1)=(E_s)_x(1)=0.
\]
The indispensable forcing is the material derivative
\[
 G_s(s,x)=2LL_sf(s,Lx)+L^2\{f_s(s,Lx)+L_sxf_y(s,Lx)\},
 \qquad f=K_{a,Q}e.
\]
In particular \(L_sxf_y\) must not be omitted.  On returning to the
physical coordinate, \(e_s=E_s-(L_s/L)xE_x\); neither this connection
term nor the derivative of the original nonlinear source has yet been
estimated for the full four-region Hartree trajectory.
