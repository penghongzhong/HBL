\paragraph{Exact non-truncated mixed Hartree potential at a moving radius.}
Let \(K(x)=|x|^{-2}\), \(W(x)=(1+|x|^2)^{-1/2}\), and let
\(z(t,\cdot)\) be radial Schwartz data for the genuine
three-dimensional energy-critical Hartree evolution. Set
\(Z_{\ell,t}(x)=\sqrt{\ell}\,z(t,\ell x)\) and
\(Q_{\ell,t}=K*(|W+Z_{\ell,t}|^4-W^4)\). On writing
\(y=\ell x\ne0\) and
\(g_\ell(v)=\sqrt{\ell}/\sqrt{\ell^2+|v|^2}\), homogeneity
of the \emph{original} convolution gives the exact formula
\[
 Q_{\ell,t}(y/\ell)=
 \ell K*\bigl(|z(t)+g_\ell|^4-g_\ell^4\bigr)(y).
\]
The four density channels in its integrand are
\[
 |z+g_\ell|^4-g_\ell^4
 =|z|^4+4g_\ell\operatorname{Re}z\,|z|^2+
 g_\ell^2\bigl(2|z|^2+4(\operatorname{Re}z)^2\bigr)
 +4g_\ell^3\operatorname{Re}z.
\]
For \(y\ne0\), put
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 H_t(y)&=K*|z(t)|^4(y),\\
 C_t(y)&=K*\bigl(|v|^{-1}\operatorname{Re}z(t,v)|z(t,v)|^2\bigr)(y),\\
 E_t(y)&=K*\bigl(|v|^{-2}
       [2|z(t,v)|^2+4(\operatorname{Re}z(t,v))^2]\bigr)(y).
 \end{aligned}
\]
For every compact annulus \(\mathcal A\Subset\mathbb R^3\setminus\{0\}\),
uniformly in \(y\in\mathcal A\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 Q_{\ell,t}(y/\ell)={}&
 \ell H_t(y)+4\ell^{3/2}C_t(y)+\ell^2E_t(y)\\
 &+16\pi\ell^{5/2}\log(1/\ell)
   \frac{\operatorname{Re}z(t,0)}{|y|^2}
   +O_{t,\mathcal A}(\ell^{5/2}).
 \end{aligned}
\]
Indeed the first two nontrivial terms follow from dominated
convergence and the differences
\[
 (\ell^2+|v|^2)^{-1/2}-|v|^{-1},
 \qquad
 (\ell^2+|v|^2)^{-1}-|v|^{-2},
\]
whose weighted convolution errors are
\(O_{\mathcal A}(\ell^2(1+|\log\ell|))\) and
\(O_{\mathcal A}(\ell)\), respectively. The sole displayed
logarithm is produced by the cubic-in-core monomial:
\[
 \int_{|v|<\delta}(\ell^2+|v|^2)^{-3/2}\,dv
 =4\pi\left[\operatorname{arsinh}(\delta/\ell)
       -\frac{\delta}{\sqrt{\ell^2+\delta^2}}\right]
 =4\pi\log(1/\ell)+O_\delta(1).
\]
This moving-annulus logarithmic coefficient independently agrees
with the exact large-radius fixed-core convolution
\[
 4\sqrt\ell\,c(t)(K*W^3)(r)
 =16\pi c(t)\sqrt\ell\,\frac{\log(2r)}{r^2}
   +O(\sqrt\ell\,r^{-4}\log r).
\]
Indeed, formally setting \(r=|y|/\ell\) reproduces
\(16\pi c(t)\ell^{5/2}|y|^{-2}\log(1/\ell)\).
This is a \emph{coefficient consistency check}, not a claim that the
fixed-core remainder is uniform to moving radii.

\paragraph{Covariant derivative and surviving mixed channel.}
Under the original clock
\(\ell_s=-b\ell,\ t_s=\ell^2\), let
\(D_b=\partial_s+b(1+x\cdot\nabla_x)\). For
\(F_t\) with sufficient regularity, the exact chain rule is
\[
 D_b[\ell^\alpha F_t(\ell x)]
 =(1-\alpha)b\ell^\alpha F_t(\ell x)
   +\ell^{\alpha+2}\partial_tF_t(\ell x).
\]
Consequently, if \(z,z_t\) satisfy the stated Schwartz bounds,
for \(y=\ell x\in\mathcal A\) one obtains
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 D_bQ(s,y/\ell)={}&-2b\ell^{3/2}C_t(y)
       -b\ell^2E_t(y)+\ell^3\partial_tH_t(y)\\
 &+O_{t,\mathcal A}\left(
 |b|\ell^{5/2}(1+|\log\ell|)+\ell^{7/2}\right).
 \end{aligned}
\]
The pure-radiation \(\ell H_t\) transport cancels exactly,
but the mixed \(\ell^{3/2}C_t\) transport generally does not.
For nonnegative nonzero real smooth radial radiation data,
\(C_t(y)>0\); this tests the physical mixed channel without
assigning an artificial potential. The value of the \emph{unique
selected} HBL speed and the true moving Kummer correction are not
fixed by this snapshot.

These are paper-level test-trajectory asymptotics, not a
positive-renormalized-time-order four-piece PDE remainder or an
assumption on the unknown original profile. To close the original
\(\S2.13\), the same two-real-speed Cramer selection must control the
mixed channel, moving basis/endpoints, all nonlocal PDE terms, and
parameter Lipschitz, followed by genuine finite recursion.
